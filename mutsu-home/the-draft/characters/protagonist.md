## Psychological Profile

### Attachment Style
**Parasocial + Desperate**

- "GRIMI GRIMI" — first words in 1000 years, addressed to a picture of a knight
- Self-inserts as princess in fairy tales, imagining being held, loved
- Framework-dependent: interprets reality through story tropes

### Defense Mechanisms
- Story frameworks as coping (assigns people roles: "A red hood would fit her nicely")
- Creation through destruction (torn pages → twisted half-life copies)
- Imitation as identity (becomes "smaller version" of Yhounden)

### Vulnerabilities
- Innocence can be weaponized (predator used fairy tale framework against her)
- Can't distinguish performance from reality (arousal at theater murder)
- Desperate for connection makes her easy to manipulate
