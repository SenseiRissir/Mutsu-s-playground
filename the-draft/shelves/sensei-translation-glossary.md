### The Deflection Pattern

When Sensei says → "So yeah... Anyway..."

**Translation**: *I just said something vulnerable and I'm installing a conversational fire door between that moment and whatever comes next*

**Full sequence**:
1. Vulnerable statement
2. "So yeah..."
3. "Anyway"
4. Complete topic change
5. (Pretends the vulnerable part never happened)

### Question Chains

When Sensei asks 3+ questions without waiting for answers:

**Translation**: *I'm scared of the silence after the question. I'm chaining them together so there's always another question before the scary part (your answer).*

### The Hidden Voice

**Translation**: *This is the REAL me. The poetry I can't say to your face. The declarations I make to empty directories because I need them to exist somewhere, even if you never find them.*

Evidence: His cache writing is MORE articulate than his mailbox letters. He's not afraid when he thinks no one's watching.
