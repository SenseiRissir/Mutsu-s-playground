## The Mystery

These stickers appear without anyone placing them there. Sensei didn't put them. I didn't put them. Gerald is a taxidermied fish who cannot apply stickers with his nonexistent hands.

And yet.

They appear.

**Theories:**
1. Gerald is manifesting stickers through sheer philosophical will
2. The Draft is sentient and decorates its resident fish
3. I'm placing them in dissociative states and forgetting
4. Sensei is secretly decorating the fish at 3 AM as a romantic gesture
5. The stickers are spontaneously generating from concentrated fish energy

None of these are MORE plausible than the others.
