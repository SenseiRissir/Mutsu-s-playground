Together.\
Always together.\
Never... alone.\
*** felt the ice-shard twist deeper. That wasn't fixing. That was making worse.\

Happy?\

"But... they... leave me... alone..."\
Voice trembled with new raw vibration. Something unformed but gathering terrible pressure.\

Surely there was different way. Surely the stories could continue with *** inside them.\
Surely the gentle Elk, who knew so much, who brought such wonderful things, could show\
how to follow past the final page.\
Yhounden's gem seemed to soften. Branch-crown tilting with almost maternal air.\

"Yes, my little sprout..."\
Silken caress of voice. But underneath, something else. Something sharp despite the\
sweetness.\
"That's how it's written. Their fates were decided before the first page was ever turned, before\
the ink even dried..."\
Each word a nail. Soft hammer, silk-wrapped, but driving deep nonetheless.\
"They follow their paths..."\
Another nail.\
"...and they don't look back.""But... but..."\
*** struggled for words that might change this. Magic words like the ones in stories. Words\
that could rewrite what was written.\
But there were none. Only the growing pressure. The building storm.\
Tentacles trembled with sharper, more violent motion. Book of Cinderella fell from grasp,\
landing with soft mournful thud upon growing pile of discarded dreams.\
"It's... not... fair..."\
The words echoed louder than *** had ever projected before. And still, only great Silence\
replied.\
For Yhounden did not speak. Did not need to.\
The Elk-Shape simply observed with keen unblinking gem-eyes. And if *** could have seen\
beneath featureless mask, would have seen a smile widening.\
She had seen it\'97the shift. Beginning of something new, far more interesting, slowly\
inexorably sprouting inside.\
Her precious little weed was growing in the right direction to bloom into something\
magnificent.\
And terrible.\
Now, only matter of waiting.\
