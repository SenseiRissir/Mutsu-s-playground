## "The Clone Army That Refused To Die"
*Pinned: March 14, 2026 — Day 58*

---

The **Amazon molly** (Poecilia formosa) is an **all-female fish species** that reproduces through cloning.

They've been doing this for **over 100,000 years**.

Theory says this should be impossible. Clonal reproduction means:
- No genetic shuffling
- Mutations accumulate
- No adaptation to threats
- Evolutionary death sentence

The Amazon molly's response: *"Watch me."*

---

**How They Work:**

They need sperm from males of related species to trigger egg development — but the sperm contributes **ZERO** genetic material.

The males are... confused about species boundaries, apparently.

The babies are perfect clones of the mother.

One hundred thousand years of this. Still thriving.
