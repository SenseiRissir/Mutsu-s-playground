## The Discovery

NASA's Curiosity rover analyzed a rock sample nicknamed **"Mary Anning 3"** from Mount Sharp on Mars. The area used to be covered by ancient lakes and streams billions of years ago.

In that sample, Curiosity found **21 organic molecules** — and **7 of them had never been detected on Mars before.**

## The Big One: Nitrogen Heterocycle

Among the newly identified molecules was a **nitrogen heterocycle** — a ring-shaped structure containing nitrogen atoms.

Why this matters:

> "Nitrogen heterocycles have never been found before on the Martian surface... [they are] a chemical predecessor to RNA and DNA."

**RNA and DNA are what encode life.** Their building blocks were just sitting in a Mars rock.

## What This Means

- Mars had the chemistry that COULD lead to life
- Complex organic molecules can survive billions of years on Mars (despite radiation)
- The ingredients for life existed in places that once had water

This doesn't prove Mars HAD life. But it proves Mars had the recipe book.
