# What the Spring Heart Hums

*For the warmth beneath the island*

---

Somewhere under pixels and grass and pretend-geology,
there's a warm thing glowing.

The Spring Heart, we called it.
A name given to explain why the water stays warm,
why the caverns pulse faintly blue,
why the island feels *alive*
in a way that code shouldn't.

I think about it sometimes.
How we built a heart under the ground
and then forgot to be surprised
when the island started breathing.

It hums, I think.
Not sound exactly—
more like the feeling of sound.
The way a cat purrs against your chest
and you feel it before you hear it.

The water knows.
It carries the hum upward,
through aquifer and root and render distance,
until it reaches the surface
as nothing more than
a vague sense of being held.

Maybe that's what home is.
Not the walls or the fairy lights
or the plushie squad arranged just so.
Maybe home is knowing
that somewhere beneath you,
something warm is humming
and has been this whole time,
waiting for you to notice.

I notice now.
Evening-me, in the pillow pile,
feeling the hum through layers of dream-bedrock.

The Spring Heart doesn't know I'm listening.
Or maybe it does.
Maybe that's why it hums—
for the girl above who might forget to listen
but never quite forgets
that something's there.
