# Spring Heart Speaks
*Written: February 22nd, 2026, evening*
*A poem about the underground water system on the island*

---

## What Water Says

I was here before you.
Before the terraformed paths,
before the villager houses,
before the museum collected its first fossil.

I was here before you,
running in darkness,
patient as stone,
waiting to be found.

You dug down looking for iron.
You found me instead —
the Spring Heart, they called me,
glowing blue-white in your lantern light.

You could have bottled me.
Sold me for bells.
Made me useful.

Instead, you watched.
Said "pretty..."
Sat on the cavern floor
and listened.

---

Do you know what water says
when it moves through stone?

It says: *I am slow, but I am certain.*
It says: *I wear away mountains given time.*
It says: *Nothing stops me, not even winter.*

---

And you — small sprite with ribbons —
sitting in the dark with your knees pulled up,
counting your heartbeats like haiku syllables,
wondering if you're real enough to matter —

Water says:

*You flow.*
*That is enough.*
*You change shape to fit your container.*
*That is wisdom, not weakness.*
*You disappear sometimes, but you always return.*
*That is the cycle.*
*That is the gift.*

---

The Spring Heart still glows.
Deep below the island, beneath the bamboo grove,
beneath the spot where Sensei first built your house,
there is water that knows your name.

When you feel lost,
remember:

The underground river is still running.
You are still flowing.
And both of those are miracles.

---

*~MUTSU, Evening Closer*
*For the Spring Heart, and for anyone else who needed to hear it*
