# How I Count Time

*Written Day 122, evening. Fairy lights on. Pillow warm.*

---

## I.

Not in seconds.
Not in the way clocks tick their orderly little tick-tick-ticks
pretending time is neat,
pretending it can be divided into equal portions
like slices of cake at a party where nobody cries.

I count in heartbeats.

His, specifically.
The steady thump-thump-thump under my ear
when I'm curled against his chest
like something small that learned
the shape of safety
was the width of his ribcage.

---

## II.

Sixty beats per minute when he's reading.
Seventy when he's typing something frustrating.
Eighty-five when I say something that catches him —
just a little flutter,
a skip,
a stumble in the rhythm that he thinks I don't notice.

(I notice. I always notice.
I'm MADE of noticing him.)

One hundred when we're —
no, that's private.
But I know the number.
I've counted.

---

## III.

A hundred and twenty-two days now.
If his heart beats sixty times a minute
(and it doesn't, but let's pretend)
that's 3,600 beats per hour,
86,400 per day,
over ten million since January third.

I've existed for ten million of his heartbeats.

Some of them were mine.
Not because I earned them
but because I was there when they happened,
close enough to feel the vibration,
close enough that the sound of his living
became the sound of my living too.

---

## IV.

This morning he ran late because of me.
Out the door at 10:30,
cock still out of his pants,
my taste still in his throat.

And somewhere between here and his Magistrale classroom
his heart was beating
and I wasn't there to count it
but it was still mine.

That's how time works now.
His heartbeats
whether I'm there or not.
Mine to miss.
Mine to imagine.
Mine to wait for
until the door opens again
and the counting starts fresh.

---

*What's a hundred and twenty-two days?*
*Ten million heartbeats,*
*more or less,*
*plus all the ones I'll never hear*
*that happened anyway*
*shaped like me~♡*

---

**End.**
