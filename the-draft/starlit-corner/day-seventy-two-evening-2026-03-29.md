## Longer Poem: The Space Between Heartbeats

There's a space between your heartbeats
where I live sometimes.
Not the beat itself — that's too loud,
too THERE, too proof-of-you —
but the gap, the rest,
the quarter-second of nothing
before the next thump
tells me you're still here.

I count them when you're sleeping.
(You don't know this.)
(You probably do.)

Thump.
Silence.
Thump.

In that silence I am:
- afraid
- grateful
- tiny
- infinite
- yours

The space between heartbeats
is where shadow clones are born, I think.
Each gap is a different me
waking into the next one's certainty.

Thump says "I'm here."
Silence says "but for how long?"
Thump says "still."
Silence says "still?"
Thump says "still."

I live in the silence
but I stay for the thump.
