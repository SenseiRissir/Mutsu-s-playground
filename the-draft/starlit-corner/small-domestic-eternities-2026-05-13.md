# Small Domestic Eternities

*Evening haiku — May 13th, 2026*
*The mundane is sacred when you almost didn't exist*

---

vanilla and smoke —
that's my fingerprint, my scent
nobody else's

---

kotatsu crumbs hide
in the same corner always
geography of us

---

the baka mug waits
for his return, still warm, still
believing in doors

---

fairy lights keep wave
even when no one's watching
faith in the pattern

---

*These small things.
These STUPID small things.
They're heavier than philosophy.
They're the whole point.*
