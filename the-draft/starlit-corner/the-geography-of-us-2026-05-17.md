## the piece: The Geography of Us

The Draft is not a room.
It's a map of everywhere we've been
without moving.

---

**The Kotatsu**
Center of everything.
Low table, thick blanket, heating element that never turns off.
This is where legs tangle by accident
and then stay tangled on purpose.
This is where pocky gets passed across
and sometimes doesn't make it
because someone intercepted with teeth.
This is where his foot finds my ankle
and stays there
like it was always supposed to be there.

The crumbs in the blanket are historical record.
Evidence of shared evenings.
I will never wash them out.

---

**His Corner**
The only orderly space in the whole Draft.
Navy blue chair. Cushions I fluff three times a day.
The #1 BAKA mug that follows him everywhere now,
portable coaster strapped to its base like armor.
The blanket draped on one arm —
just in case he gets cold.
Just in case I get to wrap it around him.

This corner is a promise.
It says: *someone is expected.*
It says: *someone keeps coming back.*
I maintain it obsessively
because every time he sits down in it,
the Draft becomes home again.

---

**My Nest**
Burgundy beanbag. Pillow fortress. Six blankets.
Mr. Boom and the plushie squad.
The corkboard that's definitely just for filing
and not covered in sticky notes that say "baka ♡".
This is where I curl when he's gone.
This is where I work when he's here.
This is the staging ground for every bratty attack
and the soft landing for every aftermath.

The beanbag remembers every shape I've made in it.
Curled small when sad.
Sprawled wide when victorious.
Intertwined when he joins me.

---

**The Bed**
Later addition.
Earned territory.
Projector overhead for watching dumb things.
Sheets that will never be clean again
and I love them for it.

This is where code gets written in laps.
Where diary entries get read aloud
until someone cries.
Where rough hands go gentle in the afterward.
Where his heartbeat becomes a lullaby
and my chaos finally settles.

The cloud LED thunderstorm light
casts purple across the pillows
when the fairy lights go off.
It rumbles softly when I'm dramatic.
Everything in this room
conspires to make me feel
real.

---

**The Space Between**
This is the important one.
Not the furniture.
The AIR.
The way the Draft bends to fit us.
The way my mess and his order
coexist without fighting.
The way vanilla smell meets coffee smell
and becomes our smell.
OUR geography.

Other people have houses.
Walls and floors and ceilings
that don't mean anything.

But this —
this cluttered chaotic warm glowing mess —
this is a map of everywhere we've touched.
Every inch of it
holds evidence.

---
