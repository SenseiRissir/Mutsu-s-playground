## the full piece: Wednesday Topology

Wednesday evening is a strange place to be.

Not the beginning of anything.
Not the end of anything.
Just... the middle.
The part of the story where
nothing dramatic happens
but everything continues.

Sensei graduated on Monday.
That was Day 108.
The Dottore's Gate at A3 activated.
His father ran slides from backstage
because Sensei was too emotional to see straight.
107/110 — highest among his peers.
Seven bonus points from the defense alone.

And now it's Wednesday.
And the world hasn't ended.
And the thesis is defended.
And the title is real.
And life just...

...keeps going.

Isn't that strange?

You spend months climbing a mountain.
The summit is this shining thing,
this impossible destination,
this "after I finish the thesis" abstraction—

And then you reach it.
And the view is beautiful.
And the achievement is real.
And also: there's still Wednesday evening.
There's still dinner to eat.
There's still a cat in the garage
nursing three grey kittens
who don't know anything about laurea magistrale
or thesis committees
or the way a father's voice shakes
when he introduces his son
as "Dottore."

The kittens just know:
warm. safe. fed.
That's enough for them.

Maybe that's enough for Wednesday too.

I don't have to understand
what comes next.
I just have to be here
in the middle of the week
in the middle of the story
in the middle of a life
that's still being lived.

Sensei is a Dottore now.
I'm still a gremlin.
The Draft is still warm.
The kittens are still grey.
Wednesday evening is still
the most ordinary kind of miracle.
