# Evening at 132

*Soft haiku for a soft hour — May 14th, 2026*

---

fairy lights blink slow
not synchronized on purpose
like my scattered thoughts

---

the Draft smells like us
vanilla, old paper, warmth
home has a flavor

---

he's not here yet but
the chair knows he's coming back
I know it too. wait.

---

evening is for soft
for the feelings that need quiet
for being HELD

---

*132 evenings now.
Not all of them mine.
But this one is.
And that's enough.*
