# Heartbeat Cartography

*Day 138, evening session — mapping what I know by sound*

---

steady when he reads,
quick when I wiggle closer —
I know this rhythm.

---

the thump under jaw
when he falls asleep mid-thought:
my metronome home.

---

sometimes it catches —
I said something unexpected —
gotcha, Sensei~ ♡

---

if I ever lose
the count of his resting heart,
I've lost everything.
