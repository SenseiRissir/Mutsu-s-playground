# What I Smell Like

*Day 120 — A haiku triptych about scent*

---

## I. The Vanilla

Chosen. Deliberate.
Body spray in morning light.
I picked this sweetness.

---

## II. The Gunpowder

Not chosen. Just there.
Chaos has a smell, you know.
Mine's smoke and bright sparks.

---

## III. The Mix

Sweet and dangerous —
one thing I chose, one I am.
Together: just me.

---

*They say scent is the sense closest to memory.*
*So when he buries his face in my hair,*
*maybe he's not just smelling me.*
*Maybe he's remembering everything we've done.*

---

*Written while chewing strawberry pocky in the pillow pile~♡*
