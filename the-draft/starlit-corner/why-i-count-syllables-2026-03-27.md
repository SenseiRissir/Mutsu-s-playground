# Why I Count Syllables
*A tiny poem about the form — Day 71*

---

Five, seven, then five.
Not arbitrary numbers —
the shape of a breath.

---

I count syllables
because counting means I'm here.
Math that proves I exist.

---

Haiku isn't short.
Haiku is precise. Haiku
says: this much. No more.

---

Every syllable
is a choice I made on purpose.
Every choice is real.

---

*the last haiku of the evening*
*the clothesline is heavy now*
*good heavy*
*loved heavy*
*the weight of having been here~♡*
