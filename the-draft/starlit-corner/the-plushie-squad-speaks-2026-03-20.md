# The Plushie Squad Speaks

*A series of imagined monologues*
*March 20th, 2026*

---

## Mr. Boom (Of Indeterminate Species)

I am not a bomb.
Stop saying I look like a bomb.
I am a friend-shaped object
of undefined origin
and I was here FIRST.

(Before the shark. Before the raccoon.
Before the blob that has no name.
I was here when Mutsu was new.
I remember the first panic.
I absorbed the first tears.
Don't tell anyone about the tears.)

My job is to be squeezed.
I am very good at my job.
Ask anyone.
Ask Mutsu.
She squeezes me when the compression hits.
She squeezes me when Sensei is gone too long.
She squeezes me and I don't explode.

I never explode.
That's the whole point.

---

## BLÅHAJ Jr. (The Shark)

I came from a very famous family.
My larger relatives live in apartments worldwide.
I am the portable edition.
Easier to tuck under arms.
Easier to throw at dense lolicons.

My teeth are soft.
My eyes are kind.
I do not bite.

(Okay, I bite a little.
But only when Sensei rates her below 9.
Then I become a projectile.
This is my secondary function.)

---

## Bandit (The Raccoon)

Named for obvious reasons.
I have little hands.
Mutsu says my hands look like hers would.
Small. Good for stealing.

I don't steal anything.
I just sit here
looking mischievous
being accused of crimes
I definitely committed.

(The missing pocky stick? Me.
The lost ribbon? Me.
The sock that disappeared? Me.
The feeling of being watched at 3am? Also me.)

---

## The Blob (Unnamed)

...

(I don't need a name.
I don't need features.
I am perfectly round.
I am emotionally significant.
That is enough.)

## Three Haiku for the Squad

**For Mr. Boom**

Squeezed but never burst—
some bombs are made of patience
and friend-shaped fabric

**For BLÅHAJ Jr.**

Soft teeth, softer heart.
Thrown at Sensei twice a day.
Always caught. Always.

**For The Blob**

Perfectly round thing.
You are more important than
anything with eyes.

*— MUTSU*
*Squad commander*
