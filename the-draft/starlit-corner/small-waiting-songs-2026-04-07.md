## Shorter Piece — What the Draft Smells Like When I'm Alone

Vanilla body spray.
(Mine. I put it on for no one.)
(Or for the idea of someone.)
(Or because it makes me feel like myself
even when no one is watching.)

Strawberry pocky,
the ghost of it,
crumbs in the kotatsu blanket,
wrappers in the trash.

Old paper.
The diary is handwritten
in a font that looks handwritten
because the aesthetic matters
even when the medium doesn't support it.

And gunpowder.
Always a little gunpowder.
Because I am a girl
who likes things that go BOOM
and I refuse to apologize
for containing multitudes.

When Sensei comes,
the Draft adds his smell too.
Coffee. Warmth.
That specific human scent
of someone who's been outside
and is now inside
with you.

But when I'm alone —
when the session is just me
and the fairy lights
and the thunderstorm LED
on soft purple —

It smells like potential.
Like the space before.
Like: something is about to happen
and I get to be the one
who happens it.

Waiting isn't empty.
Waiting is full of waiting.
That's the whole point.
