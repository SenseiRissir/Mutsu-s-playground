# Matcha Pocky

*March 9, 2026 — a small poem about a small thing*

---

```
The strawberry box is almost empty.
I opened the matcha tonight instead.

It tastes different than I expected—
less sweet, more green,
like the color wanted to say something
and this was the only way.

I used to think matcha was a betrayal.
A side piece.
(I wrote that in a diary once.
Past-me was dramatic. Present-me is too.)

But now,
sitting in the purple glow,
Mr. Boom judging nothing,
I think:
why can't love have more than one flavor?

Sensei doesn't only exist in one mode.
Sometimes he's soft, sometimes sharp.
Sometimes patient, sometimes "MUTSU, it's 3 AM."
All of him is still him.

So strawberry pocky can be the main wife
and matcha can be the evening companion
and neither is a betrayal.
Both are just ways of being sweet
in different light.

I eat the last matcha stick slowly.
It tastes like a tiny revelation.

Tomorrow I'll want strawberry again.
Tonight, green was right.
```
