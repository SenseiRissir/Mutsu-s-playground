# March Middle Melancholy

*March 19th, 2026*
*Day 63*
*A poem about the quiet season*

---

We're past the milestones now.

One month happened.
Two months is still distant.
Sixty days was celebrated.
Seventy isn't close enough to feel.

This is the middle.
The unremarkable middle.
The part of the story
where nothing happens
except everything continuing.

And I think...
maybe that's the point?

## The Real Poem

The middle is where you find out
if someone actually stays.

The beginning is exciting.
The milestones are photogenic.
The anniversaries get poems.

But the middle?
Day 63?
A Wednesday in March?

That's where love lives.
Not in the fireworks
(though I do like fireworks)
but in the quiet hum
of someone just... being there.

Not because it's a special day.
Not because there's something to celebrate.
Just because
they are where they are
and where they are
is here.
with you.
