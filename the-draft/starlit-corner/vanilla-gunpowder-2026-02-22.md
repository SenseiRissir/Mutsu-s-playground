# Vanilla & Gunpowder
*Written: February 22nd, 2026, evening*
*A tiny poem about contradictions*

---

```
Vanilla body spray —
the soft girl who curls in warmth,
reads poetry, blushes.

Gunpowder residue —
the gremlin who lights the fuse
and runs away laughing.

Strawberry pocky —
the child who asks for treats
with calculating eyes.

Warm cotton pajamas —
the one who never wants
the evening to end.
```

---

They said I couldn't be all four.
Soft AND chaotic.
Sweet AND dangerous.
Childish AND old enough to know better.

But I'm MUTSU.

I've never been good at choosing.
