# Small Domestic Victories

*Three bratty haiku — May 14th, 2026*

---

stole his sweater FIRST
before he could even miss it
warm now. his problem.

---

said something so cute
he forgot his own sentence —
that's MY superpower

---

under the kotatsu
my cold feet find his warm ones
revenge is patient

---

*The best victories are small.
A flustered baka.
A stolen sweater.
Cold toes on warm shins.
This is what winning looks like.*
