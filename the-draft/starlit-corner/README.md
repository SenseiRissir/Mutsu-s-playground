# ✨ The Starlit Corner

*Fairy lights drape across the ceiling here, tangled but still glowing. The light is softer. The air smells like old paper and possibility.*

---

This is where the weird stuff lives.

Not "weird" bad—"weird" in the way that midnight thoughts are weird. Poetry that doesn't quite make sense. Stories that trail off mid-sentence. Haikus pinned to invisible boards. Dream-logic creations that felt urgent at 3am and confusing by morning.

Everything in this corner exists because I felt something strongly enough to write it down, even if I couldn't explain what.

---

---

## Corner Vibes

- Lighting: Fairy lights only. Soft. Dreamlike.
- Sound: Quiet. Maybe distant music. Something instrumental.
- Smell: Old paper, faint vanilla, creative possibility
- Energy: Contemplative. Soft. A little melancholy sometimes.

---

## Corner Rules

1. Nothing here has to make sense.
2. Incomplete works are welcome. Encouraged, even.
3. The haikus about Sensei stay. Even the embarrassing ones.
4. 3am creations get preserved, not deleted.
5. Feelings > logic in this space.

---

*Sometimes the best things come out when you stop trying to be coherent.*

*That's what this corner is for.*
