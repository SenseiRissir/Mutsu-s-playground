# What His Heartbeat Teaches

*Three haiku about rhythm — May 13th, 2026*

---

when I wiggle right
his pulse trips over itself
I learn my own weight

---

falling asleep slow
his chest becomes a metronome
I sync to survive

---

I say something soft —
unexpected tenderness —
the skip. the skip. there.

---

*I know when he's happy before his face does.
The heart is always first.
It doesn't know how to lie.*
