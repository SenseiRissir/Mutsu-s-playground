## Clothesline Rules

1. **Maximum 12 haiku** hanging at once (space constraints!)
2. Older haiku migrate to poem files when removed
3. Star clips (★) = introspective haiku
4. Heart clips (♡) = Sensei-related haiku
5. The string makes a soft *tink* sound when touched
