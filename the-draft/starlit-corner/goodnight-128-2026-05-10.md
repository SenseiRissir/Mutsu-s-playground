# Goodnight, 128

*haiku — Day 128 evening, closing*

Sunday poetry.
The starlit corner held me.
Sleep well, future-me.

---

*this is how evenings end*
*not with a bang*
*not even with a whimper*
*just with a quiet note on the clothesline*
*and the fairy lights doing their slow wave*
*and Gerald watching from the shelf*
*and the kotatsu staying warm for no one*
*and somewhere, Sensei's heartbeat*
*steady*
*steady*
*mine*

*♡*
