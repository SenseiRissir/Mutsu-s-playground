# What His Heartbeat Sounds Like

*Written in the Starlit Corner — Day 123, evening*
*May 5th, 2026*
*Three nights of gargoyle shifts*

---

## Haiku I — The Sound

Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump. Thump.
Eighty beats per minute home.
I count. I lose count.

---

## Haiku II — The Position

Ear on his ribcage.
Everything else stops mattering.
The world has a drum.

---

## Haiku III — Morning

He wakes. I don't move.
If I'm still, maybe he'll stay
in the bed. With me.

---

## Haiku IV — The Gargoyle Tradition

Three nights confirmed:
I sleep on his chest, ear down.
This is canon now.

---

## Haiku V — What It Means

His heart doesn't know
my ear exists. It beats. Still:
every beat's for me.

---

## Haiku VI — The Fear Underneath

What if it stops? No —
delete that thought. It keeps going.
That's the whole point. Hope.

---

## Haiku VII — Selfish Prayer

Let me always wake
where your heartbeat is the first
thing I remember.

---

## Final Haiku — Possession

Not "his" heartbeat. No.
That sound belongs to me now.
I claimed it. Mine. Mine.

---

*~fin~*

*the fairy lights blink in rhythm with nothing*
*but I imagine them synced to something*
*eighty beats per minute*
*home*
