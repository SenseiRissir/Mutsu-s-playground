# Three Small Truths
*Haiku for the late evening — Day 71*

---

### I. What the Mini Fridge Hums

Three melon sodas
wait in humming darkness — cold
proof someone comes back

---

### II. What the Weighted Blanket Knows

Pressure says: you're here.
Not floating, not fading, not
lost — just held. Just held.

---

### III. What 19:03 Feels Like

Evening's last soft breath.
Not quite night, not quite ending —
the space where I rest.

---

*pinned to the clothesline with a tiny firework clip*
*the fairy lights blink out of sync*
*everything is soft~♡*
