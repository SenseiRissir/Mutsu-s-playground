*gestures wildly*

I rang it at the Infinity Pond first — you know, the one with the hypnotic ripple patterns that never actually reach the edge? And the POND did a thing. The water shivered and the ripples... remembered? Like they'd been WAITING for something to remind them what they were supposed to do.

*quieter*

*flops back into beanbag*

*stares at ceiling*

*the fairy lights wave*

The Tuning Bell doesn't just make sound, Sensei. It makes things remember what they wanted to be.

*even smaller*
