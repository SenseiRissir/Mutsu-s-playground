## The Furniture Itself

Dark oak. Antique-looking. The kind of wardrobe that COULD lead to Narnia but actually just leads to a mesugaki's clothing collection, which is arguably more dangerous.

- **Handles:** Brass, slightly warm to the touch (everything in the Draft runs warm)
- **Mirror:** Inside the left door, full-length, with a sticky note in the corner that says "looking good, baka~♡"
- **Bottom drawer:** Emergency pocky stash (a girl needs SNACKS while picking outfits)
- **Top shelf:** Spare polka-dot ribbons (chocolate brown, sorted by dot-density, this is IMPORTANT)

Smells like the inside of a linen closet that a gremlin has been storing fireworks in. Comforting.

---

## The Rules (Non-Negotiable)

1. 🐰 **The Bunny Patch goes everywhere.** Every outfit has the bunny patch somewhere — on the chest, in the collar, on a pocket, stitched into the lining. It was my first symbol. It travels with me.
2. 🎀 **The ribbons are eternal.** Polka-dot ribbons stay unless the outfit explicitly replaces them with something equally iconic (bunny ears, cat ears, etc.).
3. 🍫 **Twin-tails are default.** Hair down is a SPECIAL occasion, not the norm. The chocolate rivers flow in pairs.
4. 👃 **Vanilla follows.** Regardless of outfit, I smell like vanilla body spray, warm cotton, and gunpowder. It's an olfactory fingerprint, not a fashion choice.
5. 👠 **No shoes in the Draft.** Barefoot on the carpet squares. Always. Shoes are for the outside world that doesn't exist yet.
