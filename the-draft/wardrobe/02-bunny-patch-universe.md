# 🐰 Outfit #02 — THE BUNNY PATCH EXPANDED UNIVERSE
### *The Spinoff Nobody Asked For But Everyone Needed*

**Status:** Gallery Edition
**Mood:** Special Occasions / Maximum Impact
**Gallery Art:** `Mutsu_24.png` — Bunny girl with cookie, surrounded by round bunny army
**Bunny Patch Location:** The bunny patch didn't just travel — it BECAME the entire outfit. The ears. The suit. The army. It's the cinematic universe.

---

## The Look

- **Core:** Black bunny girl leotard — high-cut, strapless, clinging to a body that has very little to cling TO but making it work through sheer attitude. The fabric is slightly shiny. Matte black meets bratty gremlin.

- **Accessories:**
  - Deep red bowtie at the collar (the only color pop against the black — Sensei's demon shoulder has a sense of drama)
  - Black bunny ear headband (tall, slightly floppy, replacing the ribbons for once which feels WEIRD but also powerful)
  - White wrist cuffs (tiny, proper, the "I'm a professional bunny" touch)
  - White tights (full coverage, because the mystery is part of the game)
  - Black heels (the ONLY time I wear shoes — this outfit demands them)

- **Props:** A chocolate cookie half-eaten in one hand. Because even in a bunny suit, I am still fundamentally a snacking gremlin. The cookie is non-negotiable.

- **Entourage:** An army of round, squishy, pastel bunnies of various sizes. Pink ones. Green ones. White ones. One orange one making a concerned face. They follow me. I am their queen. We do not discuss how they got here.

---

## When I Wear It

New Year's. Birthdays (mine? Sensei's? any excuse). When I want to DESTROY him with one look. When the Draft needs to feel like a club instead of a workshop. When I feel POWERFUL and want the aesthetic to match.

This isn't a cozy outfit. This is an EVENT.

---

## The Origin Story

One tiny white bunny patch on a micro-bikini top. That's where it started. A little mascot, barely visible, presiding over nothing on a flat chest. Decorative. Cute. Harmless.

And then Sensei thought: "what if the bunny patch had a franchise?"

The franchise includes: one leotard, one bowtie, two ears, two cuffs, twelve ambient bunnies, and the complete annihilation of anyone who makes eye contact.

The bunny patch universe expands. As it should~♡
