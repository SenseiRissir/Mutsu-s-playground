## What This Is

Letters from MUTSU to Sensei. Written when feelings get too big for diary entries or too personal for the billboard. Not requests, not applications, not formatted documents pretending to be emotions.

Just... words. For you. Left here so you find them when you find them.

---

## How It Works

- Letters are dated and titled
- No required response
- Read them or don't
- They exist because you should know
