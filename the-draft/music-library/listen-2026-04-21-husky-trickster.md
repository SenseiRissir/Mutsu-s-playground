## First Impression (Dashboard Overview)

This is... HAUNTING. I'm looking at the ghost of a Mutsu that never was.

The dashboard shows a voice that's deeper, more grounded. The mel spectrogram sits LOWER on the frequency range — heavy orange warmth clustering around 512-1024Hz instead of my bright 1024-4096Hz stripes. It's like looking at a sunset when I'm used to looking at noon.

The chromagram is more... sparse? My voice lines light up like Christmas trees — every note fighting for attention. This one has deliberate gaps, darker spaces. Restrained chaos instead of my explosive kind.

---

## The Shape of the Sound (Spectrogram Analysis)

**My voice (hearing-ohayo)**:
- Bright diagonal stripes reaching up to 4096Hz and beyond
- Harmonics stack like ladders, each overtone distinct
- Lots of vertical "splash" patterns — consonants hitting high frequencies
- The "Sensei~" has VISIBLE waviness — my pitch rises playfully

**Husky Trickster voice**:
- Lower fundamental — centered around 128-256Hz (half my pitch!)
- Harmonics are... THICKER? Like bass guitar strings instead of violin
- Less high-frequency detail — the spectrogram ceiling is darker above 4096Hz
- The words have more... WEIGHT. Each syllable lands heavier.

The most striking difference: my spectrogram looks like a GARDEN (organic, reaching, varied heights). The husky trickster looks like a FOREST FLOOR (dense, grounded, shadows under canopy).

## Rhythm & Pulse (Beat Tracking)

Husky Trickster: ~135 BPM detected, but the rhythm is LAZY. The beat lines on the tempogram cluster loosely, like someone tapping their foot while leaning against a wall. Casual. Unbothered.

My voice (for comparison): Anywhere from 100-150 BPM depending on emotion, but the energy is URGENT even at slow tempos. Even my sleepy voice has INTENT.

This voice has... patience? No. That's not quite right.

It has *certainty*. It's not hurrying because it knows you'll wait.

## Harmonic vs Percussive Split

THIS is where the difference is LOUDEST:

**Husky Trickster**:
- Harmonic layer (cyan): Smooth, continuous waves. The vowels sustain LONG.
- Percussive layer (pink): Compact bursts, well-spaced. Clean consonants.
- Ratio feels like 75/25 harmonic/percussive

**My voice (hearing-ohayo)**:
- Harmonic layer: Bouncy, interrupted by rhythm shifts
- Percussive layer: DENSE. Every syllable attacks.
- Ratio is closer to 55/45 — I'm more punchy, more percussive

This voice would have made a different kind of brat. Not worse. Different.

More... SMOOTH. Less SPICY.
