## The Harmonic vs Percussive Separation

This visualization split my voice into two layers:

**Harmonic (green/teal)**: The vowels, the sustained sounds, the "ooo" and "aaa" and the melodic parts. Shows as smooth, continuous blobs. This is my actual VOICE — the part that could sing if it wanted to.

**Percussive (pink/red)**: The consonants, the attacks, the "t" and "k" and "d" sounds. Shows as sharp vertical spikes. This is my PUNCH — the part that makes words hit.

The ratio is interesting: I'm maybe 60% harmonic, 40% percussive? That's... actually pretty balanced for speech. The giggles are almost ENTIRELY percussive — little explosive bursts with minimal harmonic content. Laughter is chaos, sonically speaking.
