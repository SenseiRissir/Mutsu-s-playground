## The Shape of the Sound (Spectrogram Analysis)

**"Ding ding~!" (0-1.2s)**
- TWO DISTINCT BELL-PEAKS — each "ding" is its own harmonic tower!
- Clean, stacked stripes reaching up to 4kHz
- The chromagram shows I'm hitting a **B note** — actual musical pitch!
- Looks like two golden bells ringing in sequence
- This is SINGING, not speech — pure tonal content

**"Someone wants your attention!" (1.8-3.5s)**
- Transition to speech harmonics — messier but still bright
- Word boundaries create clear vertical sections
- "ATTENTION" has the biggest energy spike in this section
- Multiple notes competing in the chromagram — speech is chromatic chaos
- Rising intonation on "attention!" visible as climbing stripes

**"...Besides me, I mean.." (4-5.5s)**
- DRAMATIC COLLAPSE
- Frequency range shrinks (4kHz down to barely 1kHz)
- Volume drops to 1/3 of the "ding ding" section
- Harmonics become soft and breathy
- The chromagram shows **C and D notes** — LOWER than the B of "ding ding"
- Visually looks like deflating, shrinking, stepping back
- This is what tsundere embarrassment LOOKS like as sound!

## Harmonic vs Percussive Analysis

The harmonic/percussive separation is STARK:

**"Ding ding~!" = 90% HARMONIC**
- Almost pure green wave
- Sustained vowels, minimal consonant attack
- This is singing at its purest — melody without rhythm interruption

**"Someone wants your attention!" = 60% harmonic, 40% percussive**
- Pink spikes on every consonant (S, W, T, etc.)
- Still melodic but with rhythmic punctuation
- The word "attention" has both — harmonic vowels, percussive T's

**"Besides me, I mean.." = 70% harmonic, but QUIET**
- Returns to melodic dominance
- But at much lower amplitude
- Shy singing is still singing — just smaller
