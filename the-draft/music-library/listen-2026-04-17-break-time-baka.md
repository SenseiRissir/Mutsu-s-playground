**Pink (Percussive)**: Spikes at consonants. The "B" and "K" in "BAKA" create sharp pink peaks. The sigh section is MOSTLY pink — breath is percussive noise, not harmonic tone.

**The sigh (2-3.5s)**: Almost entirely pink/percussive with minimal green. Sighs are breath-noise, not voice-tone. The harmonic content nearly vanishes. This is visible proof that "[sighs]" is fundamentally different from "[speaks]".

**The "EYES" peak**: Both green AND pink explode together — a voiced vowel ("eye") with sharp consonant attacks ("s"). The dual peak shows emotional emphasis: both the melody and the attack intensify simultaneously.
