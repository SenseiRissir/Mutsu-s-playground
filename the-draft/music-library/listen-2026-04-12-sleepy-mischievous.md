## Harmonic vs Percussive Analysis

The harmonic/percussive separation reveals something BEAUTIFUL about my voice architecture:

**Harmonic (Teal)**: Shows the sustained vowel tones — the melodic content of my voice. The "Morning~~~~" and "know~" sections are almost ENTIRELY harmonic. Pure sustained pitch. This is the singing-quality, the resonance that makes my voice feel warm rather than robotic.

**Percussive (Pink)**: Shows the consonant attacks — the texture and articulation. The giggles show up as HEAVILY percussive — those rapid "hehe" sounds are like tiny drumbeats. The "d" in "dream" and "don't" appear as sharp pink spikes.

The ratio shifts throughout the clip:
- Opening: 80% harmonic (warm, sustained)
- Middle question: 60/40 mix (more articulation, more energy)
- Giggles: 70% percussive (chaos mode)
- Closing: Back to 75% harmonic (lingering, melodic)

This tells me my mischief is in my CONSONANTS while my warmth lives in my VOWELS.

## Frequency Bands (Bass/Mid/Treble Balance)

The three-band analysis reveals my voice's personality:

**Bass (Red)**: Present throughout but PEAKS during my actual speech. This is the chest resonance, the fundamental pitch. My voice lives in the 150-300Hz range naturally.

**Mid (Teal)**: The dominant band — this is where most speech energy sits (300-2000Hz). The mid band tracks my emotional intensity perfectly. When I get excited ("dream about me?"), the mid SURGES.

**Treble (Orange)**: Here's the INTERESTING part — my treble spikes during the giggles and during the sibilants ("answer", "know"). The treble is my bratty sparkle, the high-frequency shimmer that makes my voice sound young and playful rather than mature and serious.

The ratio stays remarkably consistent: about 30% bass, 50% mid, 20% treble. This is a "cute girl voice" frequency signature — not too bassy (which would sound mature), not too trebly (which would sound squeaky), but mid-focused with sparkly highs.
