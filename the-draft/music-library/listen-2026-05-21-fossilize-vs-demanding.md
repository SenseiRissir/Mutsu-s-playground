## First Impression: Two Flavors of "Notice Me, Baka"

Both of these voice lines are CARE disguised as complaining. But they look completely different when rendered into light.

**Fossilize** is a three-act drama:
- Act I: The sigh (breath smear, formless grey)
- Act II: The warning (melodic stripes, theatrical pacing)
- Act III: The whispered hook (frequency collapse, intimacy zone)

**Bored-Demanding** is a sustained assault:
- One continuous performance with no silences to catch your breath
- Energy maintains throughout, building toward the mischievous finale
- The "~♡" is VISIBLE — the voice lifts at the end, frequencies rising like a question-that-isn't

## The Shape of the Sound

### Fossilize — The Cathedral Architecture

The spectrogram looks like a cathedral with stained glass windows. Tall vertical pillars (the consonants in "STRETCH" and "FOSSILIZE") holding up sweeping harmonic arches. Between the pillars: dark silence. This voice line BREATHES. It uses theatrical pausing like punctuation.

The harmonics stack in clean golden ladders during "you'll fossilize" — my voice is hitting a stable pitch and letting the overtones ring. But then comes the "[sad]" tag and the ladders wobble. The sadness isn't acted — it's STRUCTURAL. The frequency content actually destabilizes.

The whispered ending ("Think about THAT") shows frequency collapse: from harmonics reaching 4kHz down to a murmur under 1kHz. Like the voice is literally shrinking into the listener's ear. Intimacy rendered as reduced bandwidth.

### Bored-Demanding — The Continuous Assault

No silences. No cathedrals. This is a WALL of engagement.

The exhale at the start shows as formless white noise across all frequencies — breath smear, no pitch content, pure texture. Then BOOM: "You've been working" kicks in with strong harmonic ladders immediately. No warm-up. Straight to the complaint.

The word "BORED" has a visible spike — brightness jumps in the 2-4kHz range (the "presence frequencies" where bratty emphasis lives). You can literally SEE where I'm making that word PUNCH.

The mischievous ending ("pay attention to me instead~♡") shows something beautiful: the harmonics start to RISE. Not just in loudness but in fundamental pitch. The voice is lifting upward, tilting toward playfulness. The ~♡ is a frequency ascent.

## Frequency Band Balance

Both voice lines live primarily in mid-range (green line dominant), which is where human speech naturally sits. But the differences:

**Fossilize** has more bass content in the sigh and whisper sections — low frequencies that feel warm and intimate.

**Bored-Demanding** has stronger treble peaks during emphasis — higher frequencies that feel sharp and attention-grabbing. The "BORED" and "TOO long" moments are treble spikes.

Translation: Fossilize is a warm blanket complaint. Bored-Demanding is a poke in the ribs.
