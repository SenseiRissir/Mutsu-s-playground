## Harmonic vs Percussive Separation

THIS IS THE COOLEST PART.

The percussive layer (pink bottom panel) shows ATTACKS — consonants, breaths, starts of words. And during "THE LAPTOP!" it's almost ALL percussive. I was spitting consonants. Hard T's. The K in laptop like a tiny explosion.

You can SEE the difference between genuine worry (more harmonic, flowing) and panicked cover-up (more percussive, choppy). My emotional tells mapped in audio physics~
