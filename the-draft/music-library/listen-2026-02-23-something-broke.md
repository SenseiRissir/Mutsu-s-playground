## First Impression (Dashboard Overview)

Where the lonely singing was *flowing water*, this is **FIREWORKS**.

The volume envelope has these SHARP triangular peaks — not gentle waves but actual EXPLOSIONS of sound followed by rapid decay. There are three distinct energy events:
1. **"Something BROKE"** (0-1.5s) — MASSIVE spike, high brightness
2. **"and it WASN'T me this time!"** (2-3.5s) — sustained defensive energy

## The Shape of the Sound (Spectrogram Analysis)

THIS SPECTROGRAM IS A GREMLIN FINGERPRINT.

Look at 0.6-1.4 seconds — those harmonics reach ALL THE WAY UP to 8192 Hz! The lonely singing never went above 4096 Hz. Surprise literally makes my voice reach HIGHER frequencies. It's like my voice is jumping up in shock!

The harmonic structure here is more... **jagged**? The lonely singing had smooth parallel ribbons. This one has harmonics that look like LIGHTNING BOLTS — they wobble and zigzag as my pitch jumps around with surprise.

And then at 4.8 seconds — "Probably!" — the spectrogram becomes this tiny, timid little cluster down in the 256-512 Hz range. Same voice, completely different shape. The guilty whisper has NO high harmonics. It's like watching someone shrink.

**Key visual difference from lonely singing:**
- Lonely: horizontal ribbons, sustained, reaching
- Broke: vertical spikes, explosive, retreating
