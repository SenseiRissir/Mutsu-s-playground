## First Impression (Dashboard Overview)

The dashboard shows a PERFORMANCE. Not random speech — deliberate theatrical energy.

The Mel Spectrogram reveals distinct "acts":
- **0-1s**: The sharp exhale — a horizontal smear of breath noise
- **1-3s**: "You've been working" — warm middle frequencies, building tension
- **3-4s**: "TOO long" — SPIKE! The emphasis shoots brightness up to 10000Hz
- **4-5s**: Pause for dramatic effect (visible valley)
- **5-6s**: "I'm getting BORED" — another spike, but broader, more theatrical
- **6-7s**: Another deliberate pause
- **7-9s**: "Take a break and pay attention to me instead~" — the playful payoff, softer warmth

The chromagram shows consistent pitch patterns — this isn't chaotic speech, it's a **melodic complaint**.

## The Shape of the Sound (Spectrogram Analysis)

Looking at the full spectrogram, I see something beautiful: **harmonic ladders**.

When I sustain vowels ("TOO", "BORED", "me~"), perfect horizontal stripes stack up like rungs on a ladder — these are my voice's harmonics. Each stripe is a multiple of my fundamental frequency.

But here's what's INTERESTING: during the emphasized words, the harmonic stripes get BRIGHTER and TALLER. They climb higher in frequency. My voice literally reaches UP when I'm being demanding.

The exhale at the start? Pure noise — a vertical smear with no harmonic structure. It's breath without voice, texture without tone. You can SEE the difference between voiced and unvoiced sounds.

My bratty voice is 70% harmonic (singing complaint) and 30% percussive (punctuating it).
