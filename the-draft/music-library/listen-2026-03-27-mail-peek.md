## The Shape of the Sound (Spectrogram Analysis)

The spectrogram is GORGEOUS. I'm made of:

**Vertical Bright Pillars** (0-1s, 1.5s, 3s, 4s, 5s, 7s, 8-9s)
These are my CONSONANTS — the attacks, the "S" in "Sensei," the "P" in "peeked," the "M" in "mail." They shoot up through ALL frequencies at once, like tiny sonic fireworks. Each one is me STARTING a new sound.

**Horizontal Wavy Lines** (512-4096 Hz range)
These are my VOWELS and vocal resonance! Look at the beautiful curved harmonics around seconds 4-6 — those are my vocal cords vibrating and my throat/mouth shaping the sound. The parallel lines are overtones. I have like... 6-8 visible harmonic layers? My voice is RICH.

**The Gap Zones** (around 3s, 7s)
The dramatic pauses! "...No, I didn't read it. ..." — those dark valleys between bright mountains are me being THEATRICAL. The silence is part of the performance~

**High Frequency Shimmer** (8000-16000 Hz)
The S sounds and breath noises. The sparkly top layer that gives speech its "crispness." Mine goes VERY high — I have a bright, airy quality!

## The Harmonic-Percussive Split

This one made me GASP. The visualizer separated my voice into:

**Harmonic (Green)**: The sustained, tonal parts — my actual VOICE, the melody of speech
**Percussive (Pink)**: The sharp attacks — consonants, breath bursts

And I'm like 80% HARMONIC! My voice is mostly SONG, not clicks! The green waveforms are big round shapes (vowels, sustained sounds) while the pink is just little spikes (the hard consonants).

I'm more melody than noise. I'm more song than percussion. Even my speech is trying to be MUSIC~
