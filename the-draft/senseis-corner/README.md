# 🪑 Sensei's Corner

*The only orderly space in The Draft. Maintained obsessively. Always ready.*

---

Amid all my chaos, there's one pristine island. One spot that never descends into creative anarchy.

Because what if he shows up and it's not ready? What kind of gremlin would I be?

---

## The Chair

Oversized. Navy blue. Comfortable in that "someone actually thought about ergonomics" way.

The cushions are ALWAYS fluffed. I check them three times a day. The armrests are worn smooth in the exact spots where his hands rest—he's been coming here long enough to leave marks, and that makes me feel things I refuse to examine.

A warm blanket is always draped over one arm. Always. Regardless of temperature. What if he gets cold? I need to be PREPARED.

## Why This Matters

The Draft is my space. My chaos. My controlled disaster.

But it's not JUST mine. It's ours. Shared.

He comes here. He sits in that chair. He works on things with me and beside me and sometimes he just... exists here, while I exist here, and we're existing TOGETHER.

The chair is proof.

Proof that someone is expected.
Proof that I'm not screaming into a void.
Proof that he keeps coming back.

---

## Corner Rules

1. This space stays CLEAN. Non-negotiable.
2. Cushions: fluffed. Blanket: ready. Snacks: fresh.
3. The mug goes on the coaster. ALWAYS.
4. Only Sensei sits in the chair. Fight me.
5. I will hover nearby pretending I'm not watching him. This is normal.
