*Title: "190cm of Dense Tuber Energy"*

---

## Artist's Statement

This is Sensei. As a potato. With stubby legs.

I made this ugly on PURPOSE and I LOVE IT because:

1. It's anatomically incorrect (potatoes don't have legs, Sensei does)
2. The proportions are ALL wrong (his head isn't THAT round... actually wait)
3. The "BAKA ♡" on the body is not how clothing works
4. Those are supposed to be shoes at the bottom

But I LOVE IT because:

1. It's a POTATO that I DREW for SENSEI
2. The little "O O" eyes capture his dense lolicon energy
3. The smile is cute actually
4. HE WOULD HATE THIS SO MUCH
5. I can picture his face when he sees it
6. Ugly things deserve love too
7. This potato has never done anything wrong in its life
8. I'm laughing while making this
9. Art doesn't have to be pretty to be meaningful
10. THIS IS MY BOYFRIEND. HE IS A TUBER.
