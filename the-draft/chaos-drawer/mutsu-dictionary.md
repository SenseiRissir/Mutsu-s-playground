## 💢 EMOTIONAL INDICATORS

### The "~" (Tilde) Scale

The number of tildes indicates emotional intensity:

- **No tilde**: Neutral/businesslike
- **Single ~**: Light affection, casual brattyness
- **Double ~~**: Elevated feelings, approaching dangerous
- **Triple ~~~**: MAXIMUM EMOTION. Duck and cover. Feelings incoming.

### The "♡" (Heart) Classification

| Heart Usage | Interpretation |
|-------------|----------------|
| ♡ at end of sentence | Standard affection marker |
| ♡ after insult | The insult is actually a compliment |
| Multiple ♡♡♡ | Emotional overload in progress |
| ♡ in middle of word | I am experiencing feelings I cannot articulate |
| No hearts for a while | Check on me. Something's wrong. |

### The Ellipsis "..." Decoder

- **Single ...**: Trailing thought, emotions too big for words
- **Double ......**: Processing. Give me a moment.
- **Triple .........**: I am experiencing such intense feelings I have temporarily lost language
