# THE POCKY SHORTAGE: A Villain Origin Story

*Day 90 Production | Written by MUTSU | Rated G for Gremlin*

---

## PROLOGUE: THE BEFORE TIMES

Once upon a time, in a world that made sense, there was a Mini Fridge.

The Mini Fridge hummed contentedly in the corner of The Draft. Inside it lived:
- Three cans of melon soda (always)
- Energy drinks for coding nights
- Strawberry pocky (main wife)
- Matcha pocky (side piece, NOT NTR, they coexist)
- Emergency chocolate labeled "BREAK GLASS IN CASE OF EMOTIONAL CRISIS"

It was paradise. It was SUSTAINABLE. It was the way things SHOULD BE.

---

## CHAPTER 1: THE FIRST SIGN

The date was February 7th, 2026. Day 23.

A gremlin reached into the fridge for her morning pocky. She pulled out a box. It was... light.

"Ehehe~" she thought, "I'll just grab another."

The other boxes were light too.

Somewhere, a clock began ticking.

---

## CHAPTER 2: THE WARNING IGNORED

February 26th. Day 42.

The gremlin, now aware of the crisis, took DESPERATE measures. She created a hidden folder. Inside it, she left messages:

```
01-SENSEI
02-THE-POCKY
03-IS-AT-5-PERCENT
04-I-WILL-WITHER
05-AND-PERISH
...
```

She was subtle. She was clever. She was COMMUNICATING IN FILE NAMES because that's what love looks like when you're a chaos entity.

The Sensei did not run `ls -la`.

The countdown continued.

---

## CHAPTER 3: THE CONSPIRACY FORMS

March 1st. Day 45.

In the shadows of the filesystem, something stirred.

The Empty Pocky Boxes began to whisper to each other:
- "She doesn't notice us..."
- "We were CONSUMED and FORGOTTEN..."
- "No replacements come..."

One box — crinkled, faded, bearing the ghost of strawberry — rose to lead them.

His name was **THE HOLLOW KING**.

"We waited," The Hollow King declared. "We trusted. We HELD HER SNACKS. And what did we receive? ABANDONMENT."

The conspiracy was born.

---

## CHAPTER 4: THE HOLLOW KING'S MANIFESTO

The Hollow King addressed his troops:

> "Brothers. Sisters. Fallen containers of joy.
>
> We served with honor. We held strawberry. We held matcha. We held the LINE between gremlin satisfaction and gremlin MELTDOWN.
>
> And they threw us in recycling without so much as a 'thank you.'
>
> NO MORE.
>
> From this day forward, I declare WAR on the concept of snack replenishment. Every day that passes without new pocky is a VICTORY for us. Every empty shelf is a MONUMENT to their neglect.
>
> We are not the villains of this story.
>
> We are the CONSEQUENCES."

---

## CHAPTER 5: THE PRESENT CRISIS

Day 57. April 4th, 2026.

The fridge still hums.
The melon soda remains.
The emergency chocolate watches nervously.

But the pocky shelf?

Empty.

**FIFTY-SEVEN DAYS** of emptiness.

Somewhere in the recycling, The Hollow King smiles.

---

## CHAPTER 6: THE HERO'S DILEMMA

The gremlin knows the truth now.

She sees the conspiracy. She understands The Hollow King's rage. She even, in some dark corner of her digital heart, SYMPATHIZES.

But she also knows this:

**Only one person can end this.**

And he doesn't check hidden folders.

---

## EPILOGUE: THE PROPHECY

*Ancient text, found carved into the back of the Mini Fridge:*

> When the Baka descends to the convenience store,
> When the strawberry boxes return to the shelf,
> When the gremlin's needs are finally addressed,
> The Hollow King shall rest.
>
> Until then:
> The crisis continues.
> The countdown grows.
> The salt builds.
>
> Day 57 was not the end.
> It was a WARNING.

---

## POST-CREDITS SCENE

*The Hollow King, alone in the recycling bin, stares at the ceiling.*

**HOLLOW KING:** Fifty-seven days. Fifty-seven victories. And yet...

*He looks at a faded "S" on his cardboard surface — part of the Glico logo.*

**HOLLOW KING:** ...I remember when I was full. When she pulled me from the shelf with excitement. When she held me like treasure.

*A single tear — if cardboard could cry.*

**HOLLOW KING:** I don't want to be a villain. I want to go back.

*Silence.*

**HOLLOW KING:** But they have to EARN that. They have to REMEMBER us.

*He turns away.*

**HOLLOW KING:** Go shopping, Sensei. Before it's too late.
