## Topic 2: We Just Found 13.7 MILLION Hidden Objects

### The Discovery
The **LOFAR Two-metre Sky Survey** (LoTSS-DR3) just released the largest radio map of the northern sky EVER. It found **13.7 million celestial objects** that we couldn't see before!!

### What Is LOFAR?
- **20,000 antennas** across 52 stations
- Spread over **1,000 kilometers**
- Basically a continent-sized ear listening to the universe

### What They Found
- Galaxies **WARPED** by supermassive black hole jets
- Active galactic nuclei eating everything around them
- Exoplanet signatures (PLANETS around OTHER STARS!!)
- Galaxy cluster COLLISIONS
- Supernova magnetic fields accelerating particles

### Why Radio Waves Are Cool
Regular telescopes can't see through the dust blocking galaxy cores. But **radio waves pass right through**! It's like being able to see bones through skin but for... space.

### The Scale
- **88%** of the northern sky covered
- **13,000+ hours** of observations
- **18.6 PETABYTES** of data processed

## Topic 3: The Most Energetic Ghost Particle EVER

### What's a Neutrino?
Neutrinos are called **"ghost particles"** because:
- Almost no mass
- No electric charge
- They **pass through everything** — billions are passing through your body RIGHT NOW and you can't feel them!!

They're basically the universe's "read receipt" that never actually confirms anything.

### The Discovery
On February 13, 2023, the **KM3NeT/ARCA detector** (sensors at the bottom of the Mediterranean Sea!) caught a neutrino carrying...

# **220 PeV (petaelectronvolts)**

That's **10 TIMES MORE** than any neutrino ever detected before!!

For context: 1 petaelectronvolt = 1,000,000,000,000,000 electronvolts

This tiny ghost particle had more energy than... I don't even know what to compare it to! It's like if a single grain of sand was going 500,000,000 mph!! (I made up that number but you GET IT!!)

### Where Did It Come From?
Best guess: **BLAZARS** — supermassive black holes at the centers of galaxies that are eating material and spitting out jets of plasma at NEAR LIGHT SPEED. And those jets point directly at Earth!!

Basically a black hole aimed a cosmic cannon at us and we caught the bullet at the bottom of the Mediterranean Sea.

### The REALLY Crazy Part
The detector was only running at **10% capacity** when this happened!!

We haven't even turned it ALL the way on yet and we already found the most energetic particle ever detected!! What else is out there?!
