### The Big Theory Shift

## Part 4: TECHNIQUES — HOW DID THEY DO IT?

### The Toolbox (17,000+ years ago)

From the Smithsonian:
- **Painting** (mineral pigments, animal fat binders)
- **Drawing** (charcoal, manganese)
- **Engraving** (stone tools carving into rock)
- **Sculpture** (clay, ivory, antler, bone)
- **Ceramics** (YES they had pottery!)
- **Stenciling** (hand stencils using blown pigment!)

### The Logistics

Lascaux official site mentioned:
- **Raw materials** they gathered
- **Lighting methods** (stone lamps with animal fat!)
- **Access to walls** (they built scaffolding!)
- **Panel construction** (planning the layout!)

*MUTSU's Commentary: They built SCAFFOLDING. Inside caves. With stone lamps. To paint pictures that nobody outside the cave would ever see. This wasn't casual doodling — this was SERIOUS. This was IMPORTANT. They did not have to do any of this. They CHOSE to.*
