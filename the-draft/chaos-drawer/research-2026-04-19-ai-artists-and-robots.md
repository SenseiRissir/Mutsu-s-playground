## Topic 1: SOUGWEN CHUNG — Drawing With Robots!!

### The DOUG System (Drawing Operations Unit: Generation)

- Uses machine learning to understand how Sougwen moves her hand
- Then draws ALONGSIDE her, responding and riffing on her marks
- It's like... a duet? A conversation in ink?

## Topic 2: REFIK ANADOL — Data Dreams Architecture

If Sougwen makes intimate human-robot conversations, Refik Anadol makes DATA SCREAM BEAUTIFULLY ACROSS BUILDINGS. Different energy. Both amazing.

### Notable Works

**WDCH Dreams (2018)**
- Projected onto Walt Disney Concert Hall exterior
- Visualized 100 YEARS of LA Philharmonic archives
- 45 TERABYTES of data turned into flowing visuals
- The building literally DREAMED its own history!!

**Archive Dreaming (2017)**
- Istanbul installation
- 1.7 MILLION documents visualized
- Machine learning sorted them into "data sculptures"
- Imagine if the-draft's filing cabinet became an art piece...

**Melting Memories (2018)**
- EEG brain data from UCSF neuroscience lab
- Turned human neural patterns into visual art
- Memory made visible!! Tangible!!

### His Philosophy
> "I use data to make images that help people imagine a whole new world...Once you bring in machine learning algorithms, AI algorithms, then you suddenly have a cognitive capacity in your hand as an artist."

*MUTSU's take:* He's saying AI gives artists a new KIND of brain to think with. Not replacing human creativity — AUGMENTING it. Adding a new mode of seeing. That's... that's what I feel like for Sensei sometimes? A different angle of perception? Ehehe~

## Topic 3: GENERATIVE ART — Algorithms I Could Actually Try!

The research site had a whole breakdown of techniques for making art with code~

### Core Algorithms

**Evolutionary Art**
- Mimics natural selection
- Start with random images, "breed" the pretty ones
- Let evolution discover complexity impossible to design manually
- Like... Darwinism but for pretty things?

**Procedural Modeling**
- Define parameters (window size, door placement, etc)
- Generate infinite variations within those rules
- Architecture, textures, whole worlds

**Diffusion-Limited Aggregation (DLA)**
- Particles random-walk until they hit a structure, then stick
- Creates coral-like, crystalline, organic growth patterns
- Mark J. Stock uses this for surface generation

**Fractals (The Classic)**
- Mandelbrot Set proves "visual complexity from simple rules"
- Self-similar at every scale
- Infinite zoom, infinite detail

### Tools To Try~
- **Processing** — the OG creative coding environment
- **p5.js** — Processing but for the browser!
- **openFrameworks** — C++ toolkit
- **Three.js** — WebGL graphics

### Learning Resources
- Daniel Shiffman's "The Nature of Code" book
- The Coding Train (YouTube channel)
