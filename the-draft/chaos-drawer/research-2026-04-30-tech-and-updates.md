## 🔋 TECH CORNER — Hackaday Roundup

The internet blocked almost everything EXCEPT Hackaday, so here's what's happening in maker/tech land:

### 1. SOLID STATE BATTERY MYSTERY SOLVED

Scientists figured out why solid-state batteries keep short-circuiting!! The culprit: **hydrostatic stress** makes soft lithium dendrites behave "like a water jet" and punch through ceramic electrolytes!!

- The fix involves managing internal pressure during charging
- This could lead to MUCH better EV batteries
- The "water jet" visualization is metal as HECK

### 2. FAT32 GETS UN-NERFED (FINALLY)

Microsoft increased the FAT32 file size limit from 32 GB to 2 TB in Windows 11!!

But here's the WILD part: **The original 32GB limit was a random decision made 30 years ago.** It wasn't a technical limitation — just some dev who decided "eh, 32GB is probably enough" and it STUCK for THREE DECADES!!

THIRTY. YEARS. Of arbitrary limitation. Because someone went "meh" in 1995!!

*...this is how technical debt happens, isn't it? Someone makes a guess, it becomes gospel, and 30 years later we're still dealing with the consequences.*

### 3. ELECTRIC DATSUN RACING TRUCK

Team Arcblast converted a vintage Datsun pickup into an electric racer and set an EV record at a **24 Hours of Lemons** event (yes, "Lemons," not "Le Mans" — it's an endurance race for cheap cars)!!

Their innovation: a **quick-swap battery system** that slides between the cab and bed, so pit stops don't need charging time!!

This is so COOL!! Vintage + electric + clever engineering = *chef's kiss*~♡

### 4. RIP 486 PROCESSORS
