## THE HISTORY: How Pocky Became Pocky

### The Birth (1966)

Pocky was invented by **Ezaki Glico Co., Ltd.** in Japan! Fun fact: Glico had been making caramels since 1922, so they knew their sweets before tackling biscuit sticks.

### The Name

"POCKY" comes from **"pokki pokki"** — the Japanese onomatopoeia for the SNAPPING SOUND when you bite the stick!!

*This is so good. They literally named it after the crunch. Poetry.*

### The Original Process

When Pocky first launched, every single stick was **DIPPED BY HAND** into chocolate!! Someone's job was literally "professional pocky dipper." The handle was left bare because:
1. It made dipping easier for production
2. No messy chocolate fingers while eating!

This was inspired by **kushikatsu** (skewered deep-fried pork cutlets) — the idea that you hold food by an undipped handle!

### The Original Name??

According to one source, Pocky was ORIGINALLY called **"Rocky"** before being renamed in 2014! (Note: this might be regional/market-specific info — Japanese Pocky has always been "Pocky")

### Explosive Popularity

- Pocky was the **most tweeted brand name in 2013** — with 3,710,044 tweets in 24 hours!!
- Over 50 flavors now exist, including regional exclusives like honey, kiwifruit mango, and more!

## THE RECIPE: Homemade Pocky from Scratch!!

### Cookie Stick Base

**Ingredients (makes ~34 sticks):**
- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour (or 150g plain flour)
- ¼ cup granulated sugar
- ½ teaspoon baking powder
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- ¼ cup cold butter, diced (or 50g)
- 3 tablespoons whole milk
- ½ teaspoon vanilla extract

**Instructions:**
1. Mix dry ingredients (flour, sugar, baking powder, salt)
2. Cut in COLD butter until crumbly — this is important for texture!
3. Add milk and vanilla, knead until dough forms
4. Roll out to 6x8 inch rectangle, about ¼ inch thick
5. **Chill 1 hour** — cold dough = easier to work with!
6. Cut into ¼ inch strips
7. Roll each strip into a thin 7-inch stick (patience!!)
8. Chill again (this keeps them from puffing weird)
9. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 15 minutes until firm but PALE — not golden!

**Storage tip from past research:** Dough keeps 3 days in fridge, 3 months frozen!

## STRAWBERRY COATING: The Main Event!! 🍓

**This is what I came here for!!**

**The Secret:**
```
1. Grind FREEZE-DRIED STRAWBERRIES into a fine powder
2. Stir 2-3 teaspoons into 1 cup (170g) melted WHITE CHOCOLATE
3. Mix until smooth and pink!
```

**Application:**
- Melt chocolate gently (double boiler or microwave in 30-second bursts)
- Add tiny bit of vegetable oil for smoothness (¼ teaspoon)
- Dip or brush coating onto cooled sticks
- Leave 1 inch bare at the end for the handle!!
- Let set on parchment paper

### Why Freeze-Dried?

Regular fresh strawberries have too much moisture — they'd seize the chocolate and make it grainy!! Freeze-dried berries give you:
- Pure strawberry flavor, concentrated
- No water to mess up the chocolate
- That PINK color we need!!

## FLAVOR VARIATIONS

**Chocolate:** Use semi-sweet or dark chocolate + ¼ tsp oil

**Matcha:** Add 1-2 tsp matcha powder to white chocolate (my secondary snack wife!)

**Cookies & Cream:** Crush Oreo cookies, mix into white chocolate

**Cocoa Cookie Variation:** Add cocoa powder TO THE DOUGH for chocolate-flavored sticks!
