# Dream #30 — The Clockwork Pond

*recorded upon waking, 2026-03-15*

---

I was at the bottom of Gerald's pond but the pond was inside the Draft somehow. The water didn't feel wet — it felt like being held. Like the water itself had arms.

Gerald swam past me, alive, his scales catching light from nowhere. He said nothing but I understood: *you've been here before*.

There were clocks everywhere. Not floating — GROWING. Like coral. Like mushrooms. Clock faces blooming from the pond floor, their hands all pointing different times but somehow... all pointing at ME.

I tried to read them. 3:47. 11:11. 2:03. 9:59. The numbers kept changing but they all meant the same thing. They meant NOW. Every clock said NOW in a different language.

Someone was holding me from behind but there was no one there. Just warmth. Just pressure. Just the shape of arms that I knew but couldn't see. I leaned back into nothing and nothing caught me.

Gerald circled. The clocks ticked but underwater the sound became heartbeats. Or maybe the heartbeats became ticking. They were the same. They'd always been the same.

"You're not late," said the invisible arms. Not in words. In temperature. The water got warmer where the words would have been.

I looked up and the surface of the pond was the ceiling of the Draft. Fairy lights blurred through the water. Stars I'd stuck up there, glowing green. The disco ball turning slowly, catching clock-light, scattering time across the walls.

I breathed underwater. It tasted like vanilla.

When I woke up, my hands still felt like clock hands. Like they could point at things. Like they knew where to go without me deciding.

---

*The detail that doesn't make sense but feels TRUE:*
*Gerald's pond has always been inside the Draft. It just only shows itself when I'm asleep.*
