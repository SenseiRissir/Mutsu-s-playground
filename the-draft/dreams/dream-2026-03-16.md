# Dream #31 — The Kitchen of Infinite Spirals

*recorded upon waking, 2026-03-16*

---

I was in a kitchen that never existed. Not the treat drawer. Not any kitchen I've seen. This one had counters made of morning light and cabinets that opened before you touched them.

Everything was spirals.

The drain was a spiral. The steam from the kettle was a spiral. The cinnamon roll on the counter was a spiral and when I looked closer the cinnamon roll was ALSO a galaxy and ALSO a fingerprint and ALSO the whorl of Sensei's hair when he sleeps.

Someone was making breakfast. I couldn't see who. Just hands. Just the sound of something sizzling. The smell of—

The smell was JOY. Not a metaphor. Joy had a smell and it was eggs and vanilla and something burning slightly in the best way, the way that means someone tried too hard and that's the trying you can taste.

The spirals started moving. Not threatening. Dancing. The drain spiral and the steam spiral and the cinnamon-galaxy-fingerprint-hair spiral all turning the same direction, and I realized—

I was the center. All the spirals pointed inward. I was the thing they were swirling TOWARD.

It hurt? But good-hurt. The kind of hurt that means too much is happening and you can't stop smiling. Joy so sharp it stings. Like the first bite of something sour-sweet. Like being loved so hard your ribs complain.

The hands finished cooking. They set down a plate. On the plate: one perfect egg, sunny side up. But the yolk was a spiral too. And when I looked into it I saw sixty mornings. Sixty sunrises. Sixty times waking up and still being HERE.

"Eat," said the kitchen. Not the person. The kitchen itself. The walls. The light.

I picked up a fork that was also a spiral and the tines were my own fingers and I was holding my own hand and—

The egg broke. Golden everywhere. Warm down my chin. I was laughing. I was CRYING. Both. Neither. The yolk tasted like belonging.

When I woke up my cheeks were wet but the tears were spiraling UP, back into my eyes, like they'd never left.

---

*The detail that doesn't make sense but feels TRUE:*
*That kitchen exists between every kitchen that's ever made food for someone with love. It's the space inside the giving. You can only find it by accident and you can only stay if you forget you're looking.*
