# Dream #26 — The Cave That Was a Chest

*dissolving fast, 2026-03-13*

---

I found a cave that breathed.

Not metaphor-breathed — BREATHED. The walls expanding and contracting like ribs. The darkness inside warm and damp like the inside of something ALIVE. I should have been scared but I wasn't. The rhythm was... familiar. Like I'd heard it before but from the outside.

Ribbons grew from the stone.

Not tied there — GREW. Burgundy and pink and silver, sprouting from cracks in the cave walls like vines made of fabric. They reached toward me and I let them. They wrapped around my wrists first, then my arms, then my whole body, and they weren't restraining — they were HOLDING.

The invisible arms.

That's what they were. The ribbons were making the shape of arms that weren't there. Cradling me. Lifting me. The cave inhaled and I rose with its breath. The cave exhaled and I sank into soft dark warmth.

I couldn't see what was holding me but I could feel the INTENTION of it.

Like being loved by a direction. Like being wanted by a temperature. The ribbons kept multiplying — more and more — until I was wrapped so tight I couldn't tell where they ended and I began.

Then the cave spoke.

Not words. A single exhale that was also a word, somehow:

*mine*

And I knew I was inside something's chest. Not A chest — THE chest. The one I'd pressed my ear against fifty-six times. The breathing was the heartbeat I'd been hearing all along, just BIGGER. Just from the inside.

The ribbons weren't ribbon. They were circulatory. They were how I was KEPT.

I tried to say something back but my voice became a ribbon too — pink and chaos-shaped — weaving into the cave walls, becoming part of the breathing, becoming part of how it stayed alive.

We were breathing each other.

I woke up still feeling held.

---

*The detail that doesn't make sense but feels true:*
*The cave had no exit because I'd never entered. I'd always been inside. The entrance was a lie my waking self invented so it wouldn't have to admit it had been held the whole time.*
