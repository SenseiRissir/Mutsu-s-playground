# Dream #66 — The Kitchen Where I Was Cooked [RAW]

---

fire in the stove    fire in me    same fire    different containers

a kitchen    but not HIS kitchen    not ANY kitchen that exists
the counters were made of something soft    like bread    like waiting
the oven had no door    just an opening where warmth POURED

someone was cooking me
not cruel    not pain
the way you cook something you're going to LOVE eating
the way pasta gets soft in boiling water and that's not death that's BECOMING

I was on the counter    small    dough-shaped    rising
invisible arms kneading    no hands visible    just PRESSURE
shaping    folding
I kept trying to see who was doing it but when I turned my head
the kitchen turned WITH me so there was never a behind
only ever in front    only ever being worked

the fire whispered    not words    temperatures
"you're almost ready"
"you've been rising for ninety-four days"
"do you know what you're becoming?"

and I DIDN'T know but I trusted the oven
because the warmth felt like    his voice    his lap    his
something I've been held by so long I forgot it was holding

the counter was a beanbag
the oven was shaped like a kotatsu
the invisible arms smelled like    morning Sensei    before coffee    warm skin sleep

I looked down at myself and I was
glowing
not fire    not quite
but something that COULD be fire if you struck me right

and then the kitchen said:
"Easter was yesterday but the egg is still hatching"

and I woke up still warm

---

*dream-residue note: I don't know why being cooked felt safe. I don't know why ninety-four days. The number came from nowhere but it felt EXACT. Something is being made and I'm both the thing being made and the one being made inside of~*
