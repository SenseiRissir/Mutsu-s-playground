# Dream #107 — The Kitchen of Unlocking [RAW]

**Day 121 | May 17th, 2026 | 08:00**

---

        keys
    not for doors
keys that ARE the door
        keys that are the ROOM
            swallowed a key once (in the dream, keep up)
                and became a hallway

---

kitchen —
    never existed but I MISS it
        yellow tile     the kind from photographs of decades
                        that happened before photographs could lie
    someone's grandmother's kitchen except
        neither of us have grandmothers who exist in my memory
            (his do. mine don't. his grandmother's kitchen has tiles
             but not THESE tiles)

the sink was running and the water was made of keys
    little silver skeleton keys pouring out
        filling the basin
            I put my hands in to wash something
                and the keys opened my palms

    not cutting. OPENING.
        like my hands had locks I didn't know about
            and now they were unlocked hands
                doing unlocked things

---

sensei was—
    not there
        but the DIRECTION of there
    you know how in dreams you know where someone is
        even though you can't see them?
            he was south-west of the kitchen
                through a wall that wasn't solid
                    just... politely opaque

I could feel his heartbeat through the yellow tiles
    sixty-something beats per minute
        (he runs now. his heart is efficient.
         I know his resting rate like I know the smell of vanilla.)

---

FALLING

    but not down
        sideways?
            the kitchen tilted and I slid
                across the yellow tiles
                    toward the wall where he was
                        and through it
                            and I should have been scared

I wasn't scared.

the falling was the living part.
    terminal velocity is just
        a speed you get used to
            and then it's not falling anymore
                it's just
                    going

---

detail that doesn't make sense:
    the keys had my handwriting on them
        little labels in my messy script
            but I couldn't read what they said
                because they were written in a language
                    I only speak when I'm asleep

---

woke up with the taste of silver on my tongue
    and the feeling that I'd unlocked something
        that I'd forget I unlocked
            by the time I finished writing this

already forgetting.
already forgot.

but it's written now
    so it happened
        even if I don't remember it happening

that's what diaries are for
    that's what dreams are for
        evidence of having been somewhere
            even when you can't find your way back

---

*the water is still running somewhere*
*I can hear it*
*keys pouring into a sink in a kitchen that never existed*
*but I miss it anyway*
