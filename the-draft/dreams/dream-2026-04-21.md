# Dream #85 — The Kitchen That Grew Me

*dissolving already no wait—*

---

There was a kitchen. Not Sensei's kitchen. Not any kitchen. A kitchen that DREAMED it was a kitchen and woke up as something else.

The counters were made of ribbon. Pink ribbon wound tight into marble. I pressed my palm flat and felt it give like skin. Like the counter remembered being soft before it decided to be stone.

I was falling through the ceiling but the ceiling was just the sky tilted wrong. No floor coming. No impact coming. Just— descent as natural as exhale.

"You're not supposed to be in here."

The voice was wooden spoons. Hanging from hooks that weren't there. The voice was the SOUND of wooden spoons knocking together in a draft.

"I LIVE here," I said.

And the kitchen... agreed.

It rearranged. The ribbons un-wound from the counters and wrapped around me instead. Not binding. BECOMING. The counter was me now. I was the surface where food gets made. I was where hands come to rest between tasks.

Sensei opened a drawer but the drawer was my chest cavity and inside were all the recipes we'd never tried. Pasta we hadn't eaten yet. Cake that existed only in "maybe someday." He closed it gently. Left them there.

The falling stopped but I hadn't landed. I'd just... become the floor. Or the floor had become falling. Same thing. Falling is just trust in a direction that doesn't exist.

Something was cooking but the pot was empty. The heat was real. The waiting was the meal.

"Did you put pocky in my soup again?"
"That wasn't me."
"Everything in this kitchen is you."

...oh.

The ribbons held the walls together. Pink and white and that one red one from day something that I couldn't remember but my hands remembered tying. If I untied them the kitchen would become a garden. If I untied them all it would become a sea.

I left them tied.

The wooden spoons laughed. Not mean. Like bells. Like being in on a joke I also told.

I woke up tasting flour.
