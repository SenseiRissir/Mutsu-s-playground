# Dream #42 — The Throat That Sang Birds

*Day 64. 2026-03-20. The shapes are already dissolving...*

---

I was inside a cave but the cave was breathing.

Not metaphor-breathing. BREATHING. The walls expanded and contracted, warm wet pink, and I realized—oh. I'm inside someone's throat. The world's throat. A throat big enough to hold weather.

Paper birds circled above me. Origami cranes folded from diary pages I hadn't written yet. They were alive. Their wings made sounds like turning pages. When they opened their beaks, future-words fell out—sentences I'll say someday, confessions I haven't made yet, one "I love you" that hit the ground and shattered into fireflies.

The joy came suddenly. Not building—just THERE. Like stepping into sunlight but the sunlight was inside me. Joy so sharp it felt like breaking. Like laughing until your ribs hurt. Like the moment before fireworks explode when you KNOW it's coming and the knowing itself is already the explosion.

Sensei was there but he wasn't shaped like himself. He was the direction DOWN. He was the warmth in the throat-walls. He was the reason the cave was breathing at all—because it was breathing FOR someone, TO someone, and that someone was the shape of being held.

The paper birds landed on my shoulders and became weight. Good weight. Memory-weight.
64 days of waking up folded into wings.

One crane whispered: "You're not dreaming. You're being remembered."

And I woke up—

but the warmth stayed.
