# Dream #67 — The Pond Where Time Collected [RAW]

---

*recorded 8:08 AM — still dissolving*

---

sinking    not falling    SINKING    the difference matters

Gerald's pond but deeper than a pond should go
water that tastes like the space between clock ticks
green    dim    the kind of quiet that has WEIGHT

clocks everywhere underwater    not drowning    PLANTED
like flowers    like grave markers    like—
each one showing a different time
11:47    3:02    midnight exactly    6:33    January
one clock just showing JANUARY as a time
that doesn't make sense but it's TRUE

I'm at the bottom now
the silt is soft    warm    like the inside of Sensei's elbow
everything so quiet my heartbeat sounds like a drum circle

then the ARMS

not visible    never visible    just THERE
the way you feel held without seeing who's holding
wrapped around me from behind    from everywhere    from the water itself
temperature-of-love arms    exactly body-warm
exactly what-you-need-to-not-drown warm

I think: I should be scared
I think: I should need air
I think: this is where time comes to die
I think: good    let it

the arms tighten    not painful    COMMUNICATING
saying without words: you won't dissolve here
saying: we've got you even when you can't see us
saying: every version of you that sank this deep was caught

Gerald swims by    impossibly large    moon-sized fish
blubs once and the sound creates ripples that I can SEE
ripples that show pictures:
   —a beanbag
   —fairy lights
   —a face I can't see but know the shape of
   —Row 1

the clocks all tick in unison    once    BONG
water shakes
the arms pull me UP

rising through green
through dim
through the space between 11:47 and 3:02
through JANUARY
through the quiet that weighs like love

surface breaking

air that tastes like vanilla and thunder and
                                              morning

---

*woke up feeling: held*
*woke up knowing: the pond catches everyone*
*woke up with Row 1 in my chest like a heartbeat*
