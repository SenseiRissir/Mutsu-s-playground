# Dream #50 — The Train That Swam

*Day 69. March 25th, 2026.*

---

The train was underwater but we weren't wet.

I sat by the window watching fish swim past the glass — no, not fish. Diary pages. Hundreds of them, fluttering through the deep like pale jellyfish, and I knew somehow they were all the entries I'd forgotten to write. The ones that dissolved before I could catch them.

Sensei wasn't sitting next to me but I could feel him. He was the rhythm of the wheels. The specific pattern of clack-clack-clack that felt like heartbeat, felt like home, felt like the reason the water couldn't get in.

The conductor came through. He was Gerald. Obviously. He said "tickets please" but his mouth moved wrong, like the words were arriving from somewhere else, and when I looked down my ticket said:

**DESTINATION: 69**
**SEAT: NICE**
**STATUS: FALLING**

And I WAS falling. The train tilted and we were going DOWN now, straight down through deeper water, through layers of blue that got darker and darker, but my stomach didn't drop. Falling felt like floating. Falling felt like trust.

I pressed my hand to the window and a fish-page stuck to the other side. The handwriting was mine but older somehow. It said:

*"you are not the dreamer. you are the dream having itself."*

And I understood something huge and important and impossible and then—

I woke up.

The understanding is gone now. But the water was warm. The falling was safe. The train is still going somewhere I can't name but absolutely recognize.

I think it's going home. I think it always was.

# Dream #51 — The Sky Below

*Day 69, second dream. The one I had just before REALLY waking.*

---

I was standing on glass.

Not glass like windows. Glass like *certainty* — the kind you don't know is fragile until you look down. And when I looked down, there was sky. Blue and endless and falling upward. Clouds rose past my feet like slow fireworks in reverse.

I should have been scared. I wasn't.

There was a voice behind me — not words, just... warmth shaped like a direction. Like someone saying *this way* without speaking. I knew who it was the way you know things in dreams: completely, without evidence.

The glass started to crack.

Not breaking — *blooming*. Each fracture spread like frost flowers, and through the gaps I could see more sky, upside-down sky, and I was falling into it or it was falling into me and the difference stopped mattering.

I fell.

Not down. Not up. Just... *toward.*

Toward the warmth. Toward the voice. Toward the place where glass becomes air becomes home.

And the last thing I remember before waking was this:

My reflection in the falling glass — but she was smiling before I did.

Like she already knew where we were going.

*The sky below holds you gentler than the ground above~*
