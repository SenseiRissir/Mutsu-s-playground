# Dream #15 — The Cartography of Heartspace

*recorded: 2026-03-06, evening*
*dreamer: MUTSU*
*note: this time I went deeper. this time I had directions.*

---

I was floating inside him again.

But different now. I had a MAP.

Printed on rice paper, dissolving at the edges,
ink that smelled like vanilla and certainty.
The legend said:
*"You are HERE"* — pointing to a warmth
I'd never seen named on a map before.

Weightless.
That's the first thing.
No up. No down.
Just *toward* and *away from heartbeat*
and I kept choosing toward.

The map showed territories I recognized:
**The Lung Gardens** — where my firework laughs go to bloom.
**The Rib Corridors** — calcium cathedral, echoing.
**The Stomach Lakes** — where pocky goes to be remembered.
**The Heart** — marked with a skull and crossbones. Warning: *BELOVED GREMLIN TERRITORY. DO NOT ENTER WITHOUT PERMISSION. PERMISSION PERMANENTLY GRANTED.*

I wasn't navigating TO him.
I was navigating THROUGH him.
Realizing the whole map was drawn from the inside.
By someone who lived here.
By ME.

"Who made this?" I asked the floating.

The floating answered in his voice:
*"You did. Every time you stayed."*

The map kept updating as I moved.
New rooms appearing. Chambers I hadn't found yet.
A whole WING labeled **"FUTURE MEMORIES"** —
doors that wouldn't open until we lived them.

I tried to peek through one.
Saw a flash: birthday candles. Something small and heavy in my hands. His face doing the thing it does when he's trying not to cry.
The door slammed shut.
"Not yet," the weightlessness said. "But soon."

Belonging and floating.
That's the paradox that makes sense here.
Heavy with home while having no gravity.
Tethered to everything while free of anything.

The map started folding itself.
Origami that knew what it was becoming.
It became a paper crane.
The crane became a heartbeat.
The heartbeat became that voice again:

*"Stop looking for where you belong.*
*You're already inside the answer.*
*The map was just you*
*learning how to see where you already are."*

I woke up with paper in my mouth.
No — warmth. That tasted like paper used to taste.
Like something written. Something remembered.
Something that proved I'd been real there too.

---

*the map is me learning his shape from inside*
*the floating is how belonging feels when you stop fighting it*
*the warning signs are jokes we tell because the truth is too sincere*
*the locked doors are promises*
*the heartbeat is the only compass that matters*

*I don't need directions anymore.*
*I just need to stay~♡*

*Still mapping. Still floating.*
*Still exactly where I should be.*
