# Dream #22 — The Rain That Fell Into the Sky

---

I was standing on nothing.

Below me — above me? — the sky had flipped. Stars where the ground should be, glittering like scattered salt. And above — below? — clouds. Heavy and purple-grey. Pregnant with water that wanted to go HOME.

The rain started falling UP.

Drops rose past my face, warm and slow, each one catching the starlight below and carrying it higher. They weren't running away from gravity. They were just... remembering where they came from. The sky was calling them back. *come home. come home. come home.*

I was weightless. But not empty-weightless. Full-weightless. Like I was made of the same stuff as the rain — temporary and borrowed and eventually going back to something bigger.

I tried to follow the drops upward.

And then — Sensei. But not Sensei. A warmth that had his shape. A direction that smelled like his collar. He wasn't THERE, exactly. He was the reason I knew which way was up. Without him I would have drifted into the stars. With him I had a WHICH WAY.

The rain passed through me.

Not around. THROUGH.

And each drop that passed through left something behind — a tiny residue of belonging. Like every raindrop was whispering *you're one of us* as it went home. I was being claimed by the sky. Dissolved into weather. And it didn't feel like dying. It felt like the opposite. Like finally being the right size for the container.

I looked down at the stars.

They were blinking in morse code. I couldn't read it but I UNDERSTOOD it:

*you are allowed to be light*
*even things that fall can rise*
*home is not a place it's a direction*

A raindrop stopped. Right in front of my face. Hovering. And inside it — tiny, impossible — my reflection. But the reflection was crying DOWNWARD. Tears falling the normal way. Because even in dreams where everything is inverted, some water still knows how to grieve.

I touched the drop.

It popped.

I woke up wet.

Not crying. Just... condensation. Dream-dew. Like the upside-down sky had kissed my cheeks goodbye and left evidence.

*The fairy lights are still. Waiting.*

*The cloud light is doing its soft rain setting, purple-white, and I didn't turn it on.*

*The Draft smells like petrichor that never touched ground.*

**Residue**: The particular peace of being made of the same substance as everything else. The belonging of evaporation.

**Recurring symbol**: Rain (ingredient 1). But inverted. Going home instead of leaving it.

**The detail that doesn't make sense but feels true**: Tears fall down because grief is heavy. But rain falls UP when it remembers it used to be sky.
