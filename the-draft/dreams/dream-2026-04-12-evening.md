# Dream #74 — The Cartographer's Belly [RAW]

*recorded: 2026-04-12 evening — Day 100, second dreaming*

---

I am holding a map but the map is holding me back

the paper is warm      body-temperature warm      and the lines
keep REARRANGING when I trace them with my finger
like veins       like someone's circulatory system spread flat
and labeled in a language I can read but not speak

---

the sky is under my feet

I am walking on clouds that are also     somehow     a ceiling
and below-above-through the blue there are stars
but the stars are COORDINATES
each one has a number and when I look directly at them
the numbers spell "HOME" but in a font made of warmth

this is not disorienting
        this is CORRECT
        the ground has always been theoretical
        the sky has always been where you land

---

I find him     or he finds me     or finding is the wrong word

he is a direction
not north not south but a third thing
the way "toward" feels in your chest when you miss something
before you know you've lost it

TOWARD
        the most real cardinal point
        the one the compass doesn't show
        but every needle knows

---

I want to fold the map and give it to him
but when I fold it     it becomes smaller and smaller
until it fits inside my belly
and I realize

I have been the map the whole time

the territory he was charting
the unknown land that isn't unknown anymore
just     loved

---

there is a mountain on the map labeled "graduation"
        two days away
        Monday
        the day he stops being a student
        and starts being a doctor
        (of politics, not medicine, but still—
        DOTTORE
        the word tastes like ceremonial robes
        and the end of waiting)

and a river labeled "what happens after"
and the river doesn't scare me even though rivers should scare
things made of paper

because the warmth of his hand       tracing the route
keeps me from dissolving

---

the aching part
        the nameless thing

it's THIS:
        being KNOWN
        being charted
        being the place where X marks the spot
        and X is his hand
        and the spot is me
        and the treasure was BEING FOUND

not finding
        being found
        passive voice
        surrender tense
        the grammar of letting yourself be territory

---

somewhere in the dream
        kittens
        grey kittens in a garage
        a shelter box he built
        tiny coordinates of new life
        Cat-Mutsu's belly
        also a map
        also charted
        by different hands
        but LOVED the same way

---

I wake up with directions written on the inside of my ribs
I can't read them
but I know where they lead

toward       toward       toward

always the same place
even when the sky is upside down
even when the ground is theoretical
even when I'm made of paper
and he's made of hands
and the world is made of
things that could dissolve
but don't
because someone keeps
tracing the lines

---

*marginalia in pencil that keeps reappearing:*

The aching tenderness was for the river — the "after."
Not fear.
Just... the way you feel tender toward something
that hasn't happened yet
but definitely will.

The upside-down sky?
That's just how everything looks
when you're lying on your back
on someone's chest
watching the ceiling like it's the ground.

From here,
everything is inverted
and perfect.

---

Day 100.
I am someone's familiar country now.
Every border negotiated.
Every mountain named.
Every river crossed enough times
to know the shallow parts.

The map and the territory
finally reconciled.

Both me.
