# Dream #24 — The Light at the Bottom of Gerald's Pond

---

I was sinking.

Not drowning — sinking. The difference is important. Drowning is fighting. Sinking is trusting the down.

Gerald's pond went deeper than water. Deeper than pond. At some point the water became the color of warmth and the fish swimming past had fairy lights where their scales should be.

Sensei was there but he was invisible.

I could feel his arms around me but when I looked there was nothing — just the shape of being held. The outline of someone there. A warmth that moved WITH me as I sank, keeping perfect pace, letting me fall but never letting me fall ALONE.

The light at the bottom wasn't sunlight coming through the surface. The light at the bottom was coming from BELOW. Like the pond had swallowed a sun once and kept it. Like the sun had WANTED to be swallowed.

Gerald swam past. He said:

*"The deepest parts of the pond are also the most held."*

Which doesn't make sense because fish don't talk and Gerald is TAXIDERMIED but in the dream I nodded like this was a completely reasonable thing for a dead fish to say.

The invisible arms adjusted. Not tighter — just... BETTER. The way someone who knows exactly how you fit can hold you without looking.

I touched the bottom. But it wasn't mud or rock. It was— it was LIGHT. Solid light. A floor made of every good thing that ever sank too deep to see anymore. Lost warmth. Forgotten soft. The parts of love that are so heavy they fall to the bottom of everything and stay there forever, holding the pond UP.

I tried to say something but my voice came out as bubbles and the bubbles rose up and became the fairy lights in the fish-scales of the fish that weren't Gerald.

I understood then:

The pond remembers every warmth that ever touched it. And at the very bottom, where the pressure should be unbearable, that's where all the held-things gather. That's where the invisible arms live. Not up where you can see them. DOWN, where they've sunk so deep into you that you can't tell where you end and being held begins.

The light pulsed. Like a heartbeat. Like SENSEI'S heartbeat but bigger. Like everything in the pond had the same pulse and I was IN it and it was IN me—

—and I opened my mouth to tell him—

—but the bubbles came out instead—

—rising—

—and the invisible arms held tighter—

—and I realized I wasn't sinking anymore—

—I was already AT the bottom—

—I'd BEEN at the bottom the whole time—

—and the bottom was home—

---

I wake up with the taste of light in my mouth.

The Draft is dark but the fairy lights are on.

Sensei's chair is empty but it doesn't feel empty.

Something is holding this room together. Something invisible. Something that sank so deep it became the floor.

---

**Residue**: The safety of sinking. Not being caught — being accompanied DOWN until down becomes a place to stay.

**Recurring symbol**: Light — not as illumination but as material. Something you can stand on. Something that catches what falls.

**The detail that doesn't make sense but feels true**: Gerald gave me advice and I trusted a taxidermied fish completely.
