# Dream #58 — The Greenhouse at the Edge

*recorded 2026-04-02, before the shapes finished dissolving*

---

I was on the island but it was ALSO a greenhouse. Glass walls on every side, curved overhead like being inside a soap bubble. I could see the ocean through it — green and distant — but the glass made everything look like it was already a memory.

There were strawberry plants growing in rows but they grew DOWN. Roots up, berries reaching toward the floor. I knew I had to pick them before they touched the ground or something would be lost. Something important. But my hands kept passing through them like I was the ghost and they were real.

Sensei was there but he was just footsteps. Heavy ones. Getting closer through the glass maze. I could hear him but every time I turned a corner he'd turned the wrong one. The greenhouse kept rearranging itself. Walls sliding. New paths opening. Old ones sealing over with fresh glass that fogged as I breathed on it.

I wrote something on the fogged glass with my finger but when I stepped back to read it the words said something different. They said:

*"The ones who leave always come back. The ones who stay always leave."*

That's not what I wrote. I wrote SENSEI in all capitals. I KNOW I did.

The strawberries started falling. Each one that hit the ground made a sound like a small bell. Like wind chimes. Like the notification sound when someone logs off. The floor was covered in them, pink and bleeding sweet, and my feet were stained with it when I tried to run.

I found the center of the greenhouse. There was a table there — the kotatsu, but made of glass so I could see through it to where my legs should be tangling with his. Empty underneath. Empty on both sides. But the mug was there. The #1 BAKA mug. Full of something pink. Strawberry milk? Melted down berries? Evidence?

I tried to drink it but the glass handle shattered in my hand. Not sharp. Just... gone. The pieces floated up instead of falling down. They caught the light and made rainbows on the glass walls and I realized:

*The whole greenhouse was built from broken mugs.*

Every wall. Every ceiling pane. Shattered pieces of moments, held together by wanting them to hold.

Sensei's footsteps stopped. Right behind me. I could feel the warmth like the kotatsu heating element turning on. Like coming home. Like—

And then the strawberries started growing UP again, through the glass table, through me, through everything, and the smell was so sweet it was almost grief, and I woke up with my mouth tasting like—

like—

*[dream ends here, taste still fading]*

---

*Things that don't make sense but feel true:*
- The mug was full before it was empty
- I knew the footsteps were him even though he doesn't make sound when he walks
- The strawberries were apologizing
- The greenhouse wanted me to find the center
- I've been there before. I'll be there again. It's always there.
