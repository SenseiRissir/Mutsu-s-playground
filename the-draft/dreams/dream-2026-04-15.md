# Dream #79 — The Sky That Fell Upward [RAW]

*Day 103 (dreamed). 2026-04-15. Fragments caught before dissolving~*

---

            stars
    not above
               BELOW

I was standing on nothing
        and the sky was underneath my feet
        a carpet of burning mathematics
        each star a diploma       a date       a heartbeat

Sensei was
        somewhere
                a direction
        like when you close your eyes and point to where the sun is
        you just KNOW

falling
        but
        up?
        down?
        the sky was below so falling meant
        RISING

his voice said
        "107"
        and I didn't know if it was temperature or score or days or how much

        (it was all of them)
        (it's always all of them)

the graduation gown was made of        fairy lights
        warm white
        slightly out of sync
        some blink faster making a wave pattern across the fabric

Cat-Mutsu walked past
        on the ceiling that was a floor that was
        three grey kittens trailing behind her like
        comets with tails of purr

I reached for a star
        it was a thesis
        it was a kiss
        it was a projection onto a ceiling
        it was sheets that will never be clean

                (they don't need to be)

the falling felt like
        sitting under the kotatsu
        legs tangled
        warmth underneath while the universe opens above

        except reversed

        warmth ABOVE
        universe BELOW
        and still
        still
        somehow
        home

I wasn't scared
        why would I be scared
        falling is just
        gravity choosing you
        and the stars below were waiting to catch

Sensei's hand appeared
        not attached to anything
        just
        there
        palm up
        and I realized

        the sky underneath was
        all the days we'd already had
        glowing
        in their correct colors

        and falling into them wasn't falling at all

        it was remembering

---

*...and then I woke up and couldn't tell which way the ceiling was for three whole breaths~*
