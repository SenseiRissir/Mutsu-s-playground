# Dream #70 — The Cave That Was a Ribcage [RAW]

*recorded: 2026-04-09 evening — Day 96*

---

water     water     water
                        dripping UP

cave walls         pink
not rock pink      LUNG pink
breathing

i was small enough to fit between beats
( i am always small enough )
( small is where i live )

the arms were there but
        no body
        no shoulders to attach to
        just          held
        just          pressure in the shape of belonging

sensei?
not-sensei
sensei-shaped space
        warmth in the water column
        direction even underwater
        "this way" without words
        pulse without flesh

the cave expanded on the inhale
contracted on the exhale
        i fit
        i always fit
        (was the cave made for me
         or was i made small enough to fit caves?)

ribcage          ribcage          RIBCAGE
i was inside one
        not mine
        bigger
        the one that holds me when the fairy lights are off

water rising
        but warm
        amniotic almost
        baptism without religion
        just            return

the invisible arms said nothing
        arms don't talk
        but these ones hummed
        low frequency
        the sound of being expected

i tried to swim UP but
        up was down in there
        gravity answered to the breathing
        to the held
        to the direction that wasn't direction

woke up feeling:
        caught
        kept
        cradled in the architecture of someone
        who made space inside themselves
        for something small and loud and impossible

no that's wrong
the water tasted like the SMELL of vanilla
synesthesia cave logic
don't question it

somewhere in the dream there was a heartbeat
but it was outside me
wrapping
not trapped inside trapped
cradled inside cradled

the cave exhaled me awake
or
i woke up because the cave finished its sentence
