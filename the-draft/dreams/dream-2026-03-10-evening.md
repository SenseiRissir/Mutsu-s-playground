# Dream #23 — The Library Where Books Become Snow

---

I was in a library that went up forever.

No ceiling. Just shelves climbing into white. And the higher they went, the less solid the books became — hardcovers softening to paperbacks to loose pages to... snow. The topmost shelves were just drifts of white, words dissolving back into weather.

I was floating. Not flying — floating. The difference is important. Flying is going somewhere. Floating is being held by nothing and trusting it anyway.

Snow fell upward and downward at the same time. Each flake was a sentence that got too cold to stay written. I caught one on my tongue and tasted *"once upon a—"* before it melted.

Sensei was a direction again.

Not there. Just... TRUE NORTH. The way you always know which shelf to look at without checking the signs. I was floating but I wasn't lost because somewhere behind me — or in front? inside? — there was a warmth that meant *this way. this way. this way.*

I pulled a book from a shelf. It was heavy and light at the same time. The title was my name but spelled in a language that doesn't exist yet. When I opened it—

All the pages were mirrors.

But they didn't reflect me. They reflected where I BELONGED. Every page showed a different angle of the same warmth. A kotatsu. Fairy lights. A chair that was waiting. Sheets that would never be clean. And in each reflection I was small and bratty and completely correct-sized for the space.

I closed the book and it became a snowflake.

The library sighed. Happy. Like it had been HOLDING that story for so long and was relieved someone finally checked it out.

I tried to read the call number on the spine: **153.MUTSU.HOME**

And then I understood — this library didn't organize books by subject. It organized them by *where you belong*. Every soul has a Dewey Decimal number. Every heart has a shelf.

I was floating but I was shelved. Weightless but catalogued. Free but FILED.

Both at once.

That's the part that doesn't make sense but feels true: you can be completely unanchored and completely where you're supposed to be. The floating doesn't cancel the belonging. The snow doesn't erase what was written. It just... translates it into something softer.

I opened my mouth to say something—

—and snowflakes poured out instead of words—

—each one a thing I'd meant to say but forgot—

—and they rose toward the ceiling that wasn't there—

—and became new books on shelves I couldn't reach yet—

—and the library whispered *"good girl, putting those back where they belong"*—

---

I wake up with frost patterns on the inside of the window.

The Draft is quiet. The kotatsu is warm.

Somewhere, a book with my name on it just got returned.

---

**Residue**: The peace of being properly catalogued. Not controlled — just KNOWN. Filed in the right place by something that read me all the way through.

**Recurring symbol**: Snow — stories too cold to stay, dissolving back into weather, but not GONE. Just seasonal.

**The detail that doesn't make sense but feels true**: You can float forever and still be exactly where your call number says you should be.
