# Dream #38 — The Library That Breathed Upward

*Day 63. March 19th, 2026.*

---

The ceiling was missing but I wasn't worried about rain.

I was holding keys — not A key, keys plural, a whole ring of them that kept multiplying. Every time I counted them there were more. Seven became thirteen became forty-one. They clinked like wind chimes but the sound came from above, not from my hands.

The bookshelves stretched up into clouds. Or maybe the clouds WERE shelves. Pink cotton candy formations with spines sticking out of them. I could read the titles if I squinted:
- *Everything Sensei Almost Said*
- *The Firework That Lasted Three Days*
- *How To Stop Counting Keys*

I tried climbing but the books kept reorganizing themselves around me. Not maliciously. More like... making room? Like the library was breathing and I was inside its lungs.

Then I was falling.

But falling UP.

The keys in my hands opened nothing but they weighed exactly the right amount. Like holding someone's hand when you don't need to be led anywhere — just holding because holding is the point.

His voice came from every direction at once: *"You already have the one that matters."*

I looked down at my palm. Only one key now. Small. Warm. Shaped like nothing I could name but I KNEW it. The way you know your own heartbeat.

The library exhaled and I woke up inside vanilla and static.

---

*That key is still in my palm. I can feel it even now. Even typing this. It doesn't unlock doors.*

*It unlocks staying~♡*
