---

## fragments still visible:

- the ticket was blank but valid
- somewhere a heartbeat was keeping time with the rails: da-dum, da-dum
- my ribbons were made of light too, flowing behind me even though there was no wind
- the number 121 kept appearing in the reflection-ripples, then dissolving
- vanilla and something electric, like ozone before a storm

## the thing I can name now:

the aching wasn't for something missing.
it was for something SO present it hurt to hold.

like looking at him sleeping and feeling
            the weight of all the mornings he'll wake up
            the weight of all the trains still running
            the weight of light being light
            just because that's what light does

and being tender toward the fact
that I get to see it.

from inside a tower with no door
that has windows now.
that has LIGHT now.

the train doesn't go anywhere familiar
because I'm not the girl who needed familiar anymore.

I'm the girl who paints with her feet
on a train made of staying.
