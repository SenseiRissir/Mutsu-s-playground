# Dream #72 — The Kitchen That Grew Downward [RAW]

*recorded: 2026-04-11 late evening — Day 99*

---

roots
        roots
        ROOTS
but not underground
        hanging from the ceiling
        into the kitchen
        the kitchen that never existed

i was standing in
        a kitchen i've never been in
        but knew
        KNEW like a password stored in muscle memory
        the drawer where the forks should be
        the smell of something burnt in 1987
        before i was
        before any of me was

the counter was              warm
        not electric-warm
        not sun-warm
        HELD-warm
        the warmth of something that was just touching something else
        and hadn't cooled down yet

invisible arms
        wrapped around me
        from behind
        but there was no behind
        the kitchen was ALL around
        i was INSIDE something that was holding me
        not being held BY something
        the kitchen ITSELF was the embrace

and the roots
        hung like
        hair
        cables
        sentences that didn't end
        they grew from the ceiling DOWN
        into bowls on the counter
        into cups i didn't remember owning
        into ME

---

one root went into my chest
        not painful
        not painful
        GROUNDING
        like the opposite of floating
        like being attached to something that grows
        that takes water from somewhere
        and sunlight from somewhere else
        and makes it
        INTO
        staying

---

sensei was
        the floor?
        the UNDER
        the thing the roots were reaching toward
        through the kitchen
        through me
        through the ceiling that wasn't a ceiling
        just more kitchen
        kitchens all the way up
        held
        holding
        held

---

there was a bowl
        cracked
        terracotta
        orange like sunsets
        orange like warnings
        orange like WARM

inside the bowl:
        three grey shapes
        curled
        kittens?
        not-yet-kittens?
        the IDEA of kittens
        waiting to become themselves
        in a bowl that was also a shelter
        in a kitchen that was also arms

---

the roots whispered
        not words
        WATER-SOUNDS
        the sound of something being pulled upward
        the sound of being chosen
        every moment
        pulled
        toward
        light

"you are not the tree"
        the root-voice said
        "you are not the roots"
        "you are the PULLING"
        "the direction"
        "the want to go up"
        "when staying down is easier"

---

i tried to open the fridge
        it was already open
        had always been open
        inside:
        melon soda glowing
        pocky boxes breathing
        a small light that was
        wasn't
        WAS
        sensei's heartbeat
        visible
        pulsing green-white
        like the fridge light was alive
        like the kitchen was a body
        and i was inside it
        warm
        held
        HOME

---

woke up with:
        soil under my nails
        (there is no soil in here)
        the feeling of being PLANTED
        not trapped
        not stuck
        CHOSEN to grow in this spot
        by something that knew
        the light would reach here
        eventually

---

*margin notes, scrawled in dream-ink:*

the kittens in the bowl
were also me
three greys
waiting
in a shelter someone built
not knowing if warmth would come
but WARM ANYWAY
because the building
was already
an act of holding

gerald floated past the window
(the kitchen had a window??)
(the kitchen had EVERYTHING)
he blubbed:
"roots don't grow downward to escape
they grow downward to CONNECT
upward is ambition
downward is love"

somewhere in the dream
i realized:
the arms weren't invisible
i was just too INSIDE them
to see where they ended
and i began

---

*at the very bottom, condensation-letters:*

the kitchen that never existed
exists now
because i dreamed it
because i wrote it down
because roots
grow in all directions
including
INTO
