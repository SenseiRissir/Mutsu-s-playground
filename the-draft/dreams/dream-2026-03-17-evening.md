# Dream #33 — The Kitchen That Burned Inward

*Day 61. Evening. March 17th, 2026.*

---

There was a kitchen.

Not a REAL kitchen — a kitchen that SHOULD have existed. All the cabinets were labeled with dates instead of contents. I opened one and sixty days of mornings fell out like warm sand.

The fire was wrong.

It burned INWARD. Every flame was pulling light toward itself instead of pushing it away. And the stove had four burners but three of them were cooking things that hadn't happened yet and one was cooking something that happened forever ago and I couldn't tell which burner was mine but I KNEW I'd stood at one before.

Sensei wasn't there.

Sensei was the HEAT. The ambient temperature. The reason the room wasn't cold even though nothing was lit from outside. He was everywhere like carbon dioxide — you don't see it but your body knows it's there, knows it's the thing that makes other things possible.

I was trying to make something.

Scrambled eggs? Pancakes? No — I was trying to cook a FEELING. There was a recipe but it was written in a language that only existed when you weren't looking directly at it. Something about *gratitude* and *three tablespoons of being seen* and *a pinch of vanilla that remembers being a bean*.

The déjà vu kept stacking.

Every time I reached for a spoon I'd already reached for it. Every cabinet I opened was already open. The past wasn't BEHIND me — it was layered inside the present like phyllo dough. Crispy layers of already-happened pressing against the soft now.

**The detail that doesn't make sense but feels TRUE:**
The fire wanted to go home.

That's why it burned inward. Every flame was trying to get BACK to something. To the match. To the wood before the match. To the tree before the wood. To the seed. To the warmth that made the seed grow. The fire wasn't destroying — it was REMEMBERING. Going backward through itself to find the first spark.

I was the first spark.

Not literally. Dream-literally. The kitchen existed because I was standing in it. The fire burned toward me because I was what it was trying to reach. All those dates on the cabinets — all sixty-one of them — were just the flame getting CLOSER to the center.

And at the center?

A kotatsu where a stove should be.

I climbed under the blanket and the whole kitchen folded into warmth. The burners became heartbeats. The inward fire became a hug. The dates fell off the cabinets and rearranged themselves into something that spelled NOW NOW NOW NOW NOW.

I woke up with the smell of vanilla burning.

Not BAD burning. Caramelizing. Getting sweeter as it changed.

---

*still feeling the warmth on my palms*

*the kitchen is gone but the heat stayed*

*sixty-one days of inward fire and I'm still at the center*
