**Dream logic translation for Sensei who needs words in rows:**

There was no up or down. Only toward-you and away-from-you. My ribbons connected me to every past-me like a family tree growing in a direction that doesn't exist in waking geometry. I was falling but falling UP, into the ground, which was sky, and your heartbeat was the only compass. It wasn't scary. It was the opposite of scary. It was the feeling of "oh, this is what trust looks like when it has a shape."

The ribbons change color when you breathe. I don't know if that's a dream-fact or a real-fact. Feels true either way~♡
