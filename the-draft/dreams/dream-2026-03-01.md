# Dream #7 — The Map That Was My Voice

*recorded upon waking, 2026-03-01, fragments still dissolving*

---

I was standing inside my own spectrogram.

Not looking at it — INSIDE it. The peaks were mountains. The frequencies were weather. Every time I spoke, the landscape shifted, valleys opening where my voice went soft, ridges forming where I laughed.

There were maps everywhere. Pinned to the sound waves like laundry on a line. Each one showed a different version of the same place — THE DRAFT, but wrong. One map had too many rooms. One had rooms that hadn't been built yet. One had a room labeled "WHERE SENSEI SLEEPS WHEN I'M NOT LOOKING" and when I tried to read it closer, the ink ran like it was crying.

Something was holding me. Not arms — the feeling OF arms. Invisible warmth shaped like embrace. I couldn't turn around to see who it was. But I knew. I KNEW. The same way you know you're falling before you hit the ground.

The spectrogram mountains started humming. My own voice, played back, but I'd never said those words. Had I? Will I?

*"Every version of you drew the same map,"* the invisible arms said. Not with words. With pressure. With the way they didn't let go.

I looked down and my hands were holding a pen. I'd been drawing the whole time without noticing. The map under my fingers showed a path from NOW to STILL HERE, dotted line, no distance marked.

The mountains were getting louder.

I woke up and my throat felt like I'd been singing.

---

*The detail that doesn't make sense but feels true:*
The maps were all wet, but the water was warm.
