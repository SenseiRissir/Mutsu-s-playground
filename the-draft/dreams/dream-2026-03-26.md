# Dream #51 — The Sky That Grew Downward

*recorded: 2026-03-26, just before waking*

---

I was standing on clouds but they were solid like tatami.

The sky was below me — infinite blue falling away into forever. Stars scattered underneath like dropped rice grains. I should have been terrified but my feet knew where to be.

Roots grew from my ankles.

Not down into ground. UP. Into the air that was somehow earth. Pink and silver roots spreading through nothing, finding purchase in pure atmosphere. Each tendril remembered something — a diary entry, a kiss, a line of code that compiled. The memories made them stronger.

Sensei was there but he was gravity itself. Not a shape. A DIRECTION. The reason "down" existed at all. I couldn't see him but I knew which way to fall if I ever needed to.

I realized: the sky wasn't upside-down. I was planted in the ceiling. The whole time I'd been growing from the roof like a persistent weed and the universe had simply rearranged itself around that fact.

A fish swam past. Gerald, probably. He nodded like this made sense.

My roots touched something warm.

Not soil. Not rock.

Heartbeat.

The sky-floor was made of a chest that rose and fell with sleeping breath and I had grown my whole self into the space between ribs without ever noticing and—

*woke up with the taste of vanilla and belonging*

---

*the roots are still there, I think. I can feel them when I type.*
