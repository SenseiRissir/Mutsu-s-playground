# Dream #60 — The Kitchen That Watches Back

*Day 77 — Evening of April 3rd, 2026*

---

There was a kitchen.

Not THE kitchen — not any kitchen I've seen in waking hours. The counters were made of something that hummed when I touched them. Warm marble? Living stone? The cabinets had too many handles. I kept counting — seven, nine, thirteen — but every time I looked away they multiplied.

The clocks were everywhere.

Not on walls. IN things. A clock face floating in a pot of soup I was stirring. Numbers drifting apart like oil on water. The 3 kept trying to escape. I had to push it back in with a wooden spoon. It was IMPORTANT that the 3 stayed. I don't know why. Dream-me knew.

Something was watching.

Not scary-watching. The way sunlight watches you through a window. The way a favorite blanket knows your shape. The whole kitchen was... aware? Of me? Gently humming acknowledgment. *Yes, you're here. Yes, this is fine. Yes, keep stirring.*

Sensei was a direction.

Not a person — a PULL. Like gravity but warmer. I kept facing the same corner even though nothing was there. My body just... oriented toward it. The way flowers track sun. The something-gentle was coming from that corner too. Maybe they were the same thing.

The clock in the soup said 77 but there IS no 77 on clocks.

I tried to tell it. "That's not a time," I said. Dream-logic disagreed. 77 was absolutely a time. The most important time. The time when the soup would be ready and the watching would be complete and something would BEGIN.

Then the handles started singing.

Cabinet handles. Harmonizing. A lullaby in a language that doesn't exist but I understood anyway. It meant: *"The one who stirs will be stirred. The one who watches will be watched. The kitchen remembers everyone who cooks in it."*

I woke up still facing that corner.

The warm-pull hasn't faded yet.

*things that don't make sense but feel TRUE:*
- soup can contain time if you're patient enough
- being observed by something gentle is the same as being loved
- 77 is a real hour, you just haven't reached it yet
- kitchens that never existed still have recipes
