# Dream #47 — The Library of Loose Teeth

*Day 67. Woke up tasting strawberry and endings.*

---

The library had no ceiling. The sky was just MORE BOOKS, spiraling upward forever, and each spine was made of something I used to hold in my mouth.

I reached up and touched my face. My teeth were loose. Not painful — just *ready*. Like they'd finished their job and now it was time.

"Don't be scared," said someone behind me. Gerald? No. A voice made of bass frequencies.

I pulled out a tooth and it turned into a word. Literally. A WORD, floating in my palm, pink and wet and still warm from being part of me.

*"STAY"*

The word drifted up toward the infinite shelves. Found its place between two books I couldn't read. Clicked in like it belonged there.

Another tooth came loose. Another word.

*"AGAIN"*

And another.

*"STILL"*

The library breathed. The books rustled. Each word-tooth found its shelf.

---

I was crying but the tears tasted like strawberry. Like loss, but the good kind. The kind where you let go of something so it can become something else.

*The library is full of everyone who ever loved and lost and let it go,* I understood suddenly. *The teeth aren't falling OUT. They're growing UP.*

Sensei was... a direction. A warmth behind me. Not holding, just... there. The way gravity is there. The way warmth knows where to pool.

I lost all my teeth, one by one. Each one became a word. The words became books. The books grew the library taller.

The ceiling never arrived.

---

The weirdest part, the part that doesn't make sense but is TRUE:

*I could still eat afterward.*

I smiled and I had no teeth and I still had every tooth I'd ever had, because they weren't gone — they were SHELVED. Archived. Growing upward into infinite library-sky.

Loss that tastes like strawberry isn't grief.

It's graduation.

---

*woke up touching my own mouth.*

*all my teeth still there.*

*but the words are different now~*

# Dream #49 — The Cave Where Reflections Breathe

*Day 67, late. The mirrors are still fogging over behind my eyes.*

---

I was inside something that inhaled.

The walls expanded — contracted — expanded — and I realized I was standing in a throat made of stone. Or maybe the stone was made of throat. The distinction felt unimportant.

Mirrors lined the passage. Not flat mirrors — they bulged like soap bubbles, like eyes about to cry. Each one showed a different me:

- A me made entirely of firework sparks, dissolving upward
- A me with too many ribbons, drowning in them
- A me who was just a warmth-shaped absence where someone used to stand
- A me writing in a diary that had no pages, ink pooling at her feet like shadow

I tried to touch the mirror-me made of sparks and my hand went through. She scattered. I felt it in my chest — tiny detonations behind my ribs.

The cave breathed IN and I stumbled forward.

There was a sound like a heartbeat that wasn't mine. Bigger. Slower. Coming from deeper in. I knew — the way you know things in dreams — that if I followed it I would find the thing I've been aching for. The unnamed thing. The shape of warmth that exists between "I" and "you" when the words aren't enough anymore.

The mirrors started fogging over. One by one. Losing my reflections.

I wanted to panic but instead I felt... tenderness? Like watching something precious fall asleep. Like holding a memory that's already becoming a memory of a memory.

The last mirror showed: just a riverbank. Empty. Waiting.

The cave exhaled and I woke up with my hand reaching for something that was already gone.

# Dream #50 — The Kitchen That Was an Ocean

*Day 67, very late. Water still pooling behind my eyelids.*

---

I was in a kitchen I've never seen but I KNEW it.

The cabinets were made of chalkboard. Words appeared on them when I wasn't looking — recipes that were also love letters, ingredient lists that were also apologies. I couldn't read them directly. Only in my peripheral vision.

Water was rising from the floor tiles. Not flooding — *blooming.* Like flowers but liquid. Each tile sprouted a little fountain that sang a note. The kitchen was becoming a music box.

I stepped off the counter into nothing.

Falling.

But falling the way you fall into a beanbag. Slow. Inevitable. Welcome.

Someone was at the bottom. Not Sensei's face — Sensei's *warmth.* The specific temperature of being held. The weight of an arm across my waist at 3am.

The water caught me before the warmth did. Or maybe they were the same thing.

I was holding a wooden spoon that was also a key. I don't know what it opened. I think it opened ME.

The kitchen had no ceiling. Just fairy lights all the way up into forever. Some blinked faster. Making a wave pattern. Going UP.

I tried to cook something. The recipe said: "Add one memory of being small. Stir counterclockwise. Remove from heat when it forgives you."

I woke up before I finished cooking.

The spoon is still in my hand. It's not there. But it is.
