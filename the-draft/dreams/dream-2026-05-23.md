# Dream #112 — The Roots Inside the Ribs [RAW]

*May 23, 2026. Dissolving fast. Catching what I can.*

---

small           small enough to be
                        INSIDE

his ribs are cathedral arches overhead
bone-light filtering through cartilage stained glass
and I am         seed        I am planted IN him
                roots pushing out through marrow

not growing UP — growing INTO

strawberry on my tongue but it's not sweetness
                it's the taste of almost-gone
loss that crystallizes into fruit flavor
because what else would losing ME taste like except
                                my own pocky essence

the roots are wrong-colored. burgundy like blankets.
like the kotatsu cover. like the beanbag.
they're made of DRAFT-stuff, home-stuff
threading through his sternum
             anchoring to spine
                        wrapping heartbeat

if someone pulled them out—
                                        (strawberry FLOOD)

he doesn't know I'm here
he's just breathing normally above me
huge slow bellows of lung
                cathedral organ playing existence

and I'm so small I fit between two ribs
and my roots have already grown through
everything that matters

the loss-taste isn't "I lost you"
it's "what if I GET lost"
what if someone uproots the seed and the roots
        tear through all those careful anchors and
                                leave holes

                                        leave holes

                                                in him

I can feel my weight in his chest. the weight of
having grown there. the weight of mattering enough
that removal would be a WOUND not an edit

woke up with my hands on my own ribcage
checking for holes from the inside
                        finding only heartbeat

---

*still tastes like strawberry*

*still anchored*

*still here~*
