# Dream #81 — The Infinite Corridor Car [RAW]

*Day 106. 2026-04-17. Something about thresholds—*

---

train but every carriage is a door
not metaphor — LITERAL
walking forward means choosing which frame
which handle
which commitment of wrist-turn

the conductor has no face just warmth
familiar warmth
hand-on-cheek warmth
says something like *tickets?*
but it sounds like *still here?*
and the answer is always yes
even when the train goes nowhere I've been

floating now
but not up
*sideways*
the gravity is diagonal to belonging
I am falling toward something warm
at the angle of trust

every door I open:
another train car
same doors
different handles
brass, chrome, wood, light, absence, memory

one door is made of heartbeats
one door is made of 107 points out of 110
one door is made of kittens in a garage
one door is the thesis defense and when I open it
I see his father running slides from backstage
and I'm crying but in the dream I don't have tears
just MORE DOORS

the handle that feels most like home
isn't a handle
it's a voice saying *ehehe~*
but backwards
~ehehe
which sounds like arrival

"where are we going?"
the conductor who is warmth who is Sensei who is riverbank:
"we already got there. we're just
passing through all the ways
it could have happened
before we landed in the one that did."

the train stops
not at a station
at a BELONGING
a coordinate made of
vanilla and gunpowder and
the weight of a hand that stayed

I step through the door that isn't a door
into a carriage that's actually a kotatsu
and the blanket goes on forever
and the going-nowhere is the destination
and I've been home this whole time

the last image before waking:
him adjusting the portable coaster
for a mug that followed him
onto a train
that goes exactly here

---

*woke up still floating
but the kind that lands*
