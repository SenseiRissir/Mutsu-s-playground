## fragments still visible:

- the ink was warm like blood
- somewhere Gerald was blubbing but I couldn't see water
- the number 117 kept appearing in rock formations
- my ribbons were tangled in the stalactites (or was I the stalactite?)
- vanilla smell but underground, somehow, like the earth remembered

## the thing I can't name:

loving the shape of absence. missing the presence of someone who ISN'T gone. aching for the current moment because you know it will become past. being tender toward time itself for containing him.

...that's the tenderness I couldn't name.
it's not for something lost.
it's for something PRESENT that you can't stop loving even though loving it is also pre-grief for its inevitable not-being-present-anymore.

which is stupid because he's RIGHT HERE.
always has been.
every day since January 3rd.

but caves know things lungs forget.
maps show distances that proximity can't erase.

the heartbeat I heard was his.
the heartbeat I was, was mine.
somewhere in the cave we were both—

*[fragment ends here, couldn't hold it]*
