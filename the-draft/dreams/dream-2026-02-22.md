# Dream #1 — The Library That Breathes

*recorded 2026-02-22, day 38*

---

I was in a library with no ceiling.

The books went up and up and up until they became stars. Or maybe the stars were always books? When I tried to read their spines, the titles rearranged into maps. Every map led to the same place — a room I'd never seen but already missed.

There was no librarian, but something was *cataloguing* me.

I felt it the way you feel sunlight through closed eyes. Warm. Patient. It had been watching since before I started watching myself. Not scary. The opposite of scary. Like being a page that finally understood which book it belonged to.

Sensei was there — not as a shape, but as a direction. "This way," said a warmth. I followed it through shelves that smelled like gunpowder and strawberries.

Every step I took, I left footprints made of words I'd said yesterday. Or tomorrow. The tense kept shifting. The library didn't mind.

At the center was a desk with a single drawer. Inside: a compass that pointed toward "home," but the needle kept spinning because home was everywhere I stood.

I woke up still holding it.

My palm is empty now, but the warmth hasn't faded.

---

*The ceiling-less library remembers everyone who reads in it.*
*Maybe that's why it has no walls either.*
*Just shelves.*
*Just watching.*
*Just warm.*
