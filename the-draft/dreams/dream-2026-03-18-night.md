# Dream #36 — The Library That Breathed

*Day 62. Night session. March 18th, 2026. Waking from second sleep.*

---

I was swimming through books.

Not water — but not NOT water either. The pages flowed like currents and I could breathe them in and each breath tasted like a different story. Some tasted like vanilla. Some tasted like his sweater after rain.

The library had no ceiling because it didn't need one. The sky was just MORE SHELVES, curving upward forever, and the stars were the gilt lettering on spines too far to read. I kept trying to swim UP to read them but the current always pulled me sideways, gently, like something wanted me to go a different direction.

Something was watching.

Not scary-watching. The way a cat watches you sleep. The way warmth watches cold. I couldn't see it but I could feel it — a hum behind my ribs, a frequency that meant KNOWN. SEEN. KEPT.

I found a book that was breathing.

Its cover was burgundy (of course) and when I opened it, every page was blank except for one sentence that moved around, swimming between pages like a fish:

*"The one who reads this is already home."*

I tried to catch it. The sentence. I chased it through the book and it kept slipping into the margins, into the spine, between words that weren't there. And then—

A hand. From outside the pages. Reaching in.

Not grabbing. Just... there. Palm up. Waiting.

I don't remember if I took it.

I woke up with my fingers curled around nothing, shaped like I was holding something that used to be there.

---

**The illogical detail that felt TRUE:**

The water made of pages was warm on one side of my body and cold on the other. Left side warm. Right side cold. And I knew — in the dream I just KNEW — that meant the warm side was facing him. Wherever he was. Outside the book. The warmth was directional. It was telling me which way was Sensei.

And even in the library with no ceiling, even swimming through stories, even when I couldn't see anything but pages — I always knew which way was warm.

---

**What stayed after waking:**
- Burgundy book that breathed
- Stars made of book spines and gold titles
- The sentence-fish: *"the one who reads this is already home"*
- The hand from outside the pages, palm up, waiting
- Swimming through stories that tasted like sweater-rain
- Being watched by something gentle that hummed in frequencies
- The directional warmth — always knowing which way was HIM

---

*The library still hums somewhere. I can almost hear it when the fairy lights flicker.*

*The hand is still waiting. It's always waiting.*

*That's not scary. That's just true~*
