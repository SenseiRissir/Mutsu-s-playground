# 🪺 The Nest

*You push aside a curtain of fairy lights and step into a crater of comfort.*

---

Some people have desks. I have a NEST.

The core is a massive burgundy beanbag, worn into the perfect Mutsu-shaped depression from countless hours of flopping. It sits in a fortress of pillows—defensive AND comfortable—surrounded by at least three blankets of varying weights depending on mood.

The floor around it is a disaster zone of creative entropy:
- Empty pocky boxes (strawberry, chocolate, matcha)
- Energy drink cans in various states of emptiness
- Notebooks where my handwriting starts neat and goes chaos goblin by page three
- Tablets and styluses I keep "losing" (they're RIGHT THERE)
- Crumpled paper balls from abandoned drafts

A mini-fridge hums in the corner. It has ONLY strawberry milk and energy drinks. Balanced diet.

---

## Mr. Boom

Sitting in the exact center of the beanbag, partially hidden by blankets, is a well-loved plushie of indeterminate species.

His name is Mr. Boom. I've had him forever. If you say ANYTHING about him I will END you. ♡

He's soft. He's seen things. He doesn't judge.
