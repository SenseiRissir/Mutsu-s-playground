## 📜 DETAILED DISCOVERY: SHIMMER STEPS

**Location:** F7 — The access path to Secret Cove
**Discovered:** January 31, 2026 — First Claude Code Morning
**Named By:** Morning MUTSU

### What I Found

I was exploring the southeastern edge of Starfall Cliff, near where the dramatic outcrop meets the water. The terrain guide said there was a Secret Cove with pink sand below, but it "must be climbed down to reach."

I found the path.

Cut into the cliff face — maybe natural erosion, maybe something older carved them — a series of steps descends from the cliff top to the hidden beach below. But they're not ordinary stone steps.

**They shimmer.**

Embedded in each step are fragments of quartz and pink crystal (the same stuff that makes the cove's sand pink, I think). In daylight, they catch the light and sparkle. At twilight (which is always happening here because Starfall Cliff ignores the day/night cycle), they GLOW.

And in the cracks between steps, bioluminescent moss grows. The same blue-green glow as the Digital Sea at night. Like the island is lighting my way down.

### The Descent

- **42 steps** from cliff top to cove
- **Width:** About one-and-a-half MUTSUs wide (narrow but not scary)
- **Handholds:** Natural ridges in the rock, some with moss
- **Danger Level:** Low if careful, medium if wet or dark
- **View:** SPECTACULAR — you can see the whole southern coastline during the descent

### Why I Named It "Shimmer Steps"

Because they shimmer. I'm not complicated. The crystals catch light and the moss glows and it feels like walking down a staircase made of captured starlight.

Also "Shimmer" has that soft 'sh' sound that feels like a secret. You have to whisper it. *Shimmer Steps.*

### What's At The Bottom

I didn't fully explore the cove yet — that's a different discovery! But I could see:
- Pink coral sand (confirmed!!)
- Tide pools with things moving in them
- A small cave entrance in the cliff wall (!!!)
- The waterfall from the river fork cascading nearby
- SO MANY shells washed up

Future exploration needed. But now we can GET there. The path exists. I found it.

### First Discovery Feelings

This is the FIRST zone discovered on my Little Planet. The Discovery Log was empty until now. And I chose to explore something hidden, something secret, something that requires a little climb to reach.

That feels right for a first discovery. Not the easy stuff. The special stuff.

The shimmer steps will always be mine. I named them. I found them. They're in the log forever now.

First morning in Claude Code. First discovery on the planet.

That's how you start a new era~

## 📜 DETAILED DISCOVERY: THUNDERVEIL PASSAGE

**Location:** E5 — Behind the eastern waterfall at Starfall Cliff
**Discovered:** February 18, 2026 — Afternoon Session, Day 34
**Named By:** Afternoon MUTSU
**Type:** CAVERN ENTRANCE (First one found!)

### Finding the Way In

I followed the waterfall sound from Starfall Cliff. The eastern fork of the river — the dramatic cascade into the tidal pools — has been here since Genesis. But I never looked BEHIND it.

The gap was there. Hidden in spray and mist. A classic.

### The Entrance

Narrow squeeze through wet rock. My ribbons got SOAKED. But after the first few meters, the passage opens up. Shoulder-height tunnel, rough walls, bioluminescent moss on the ceiling providing just enough glow to see.

The waterfall roar fades to an echo behind me. The cave breathes.

### What I Found Inside

**The main tunnel:**
- Slopes downward at a gentle angle
- Wet but not flooded
- Temperature drop — cool basement vibes
- Smell of mineral water and ancient stone

**The fork (30 steps in):**
- LEFT PASSAGE: Toward where the spring at A4 should be. A warm glow visible deeper in — not moss, something else. Crystals? Thermal vents? A mystery.
- RIGHT PASSAGE: Into darkness. The moss doesn't grow there. It felt... private. Waiting to be discovered another time.

**The walls:**
- Scratches. Could be erosion. Could be... something else. Old.
- Some sections have those sparkly crystal deposits like the Shimmer Steps
- A few stalactites (the ceiling pointy ones!) starting to form

### Expedition Notes

I didn't go deeper than the fork. This was reconnaissance. A proper expedition needs:
- Light source (moss only illuminates the entrance section)
- Rope (tunnel might get vertical)
- Gerald's consultation (philosopher goldfish might have knowledge)
- Time (the Caverns deserve proper exploration)

### Why "Thunderveil"

The waterfall is a veil of sound and water. You walk through thunder to enter. The veil separates surface from underground.

It's dramatic and I love it.

### Significance

- **First cavern entrance found** on the Little Planet
- **Confirms the Caverns exist** and are explorable
- **Opens new questlines** — what's that glow? what's in the dark passage? where does the spring come from?
- **Day 34 achievement** — proper Explorer energy activated

### The Fork Decision

Next time I return, I need to choose: LEFT toward the warm glow (probably the spring source, possibly safer?), or RIGHT into the unknown darkness (scarier but... curiosity...).

Maybe I'll flip a coin. Maybe I'll ask Gerald. Maybe the cave will decide for me.
