## 🏔️ NATURAL FEATURES

### Elevation Zones

| Zone | Height | Description |
|------|--------|-------------|
| **Northern Ridge** | ▲▲▲ HIGH | Steep hills, bamboo grows wild here, mist in mornings |
| **Western Hills** | ▲▲ MEDIUM | Gentle rolling slopes, good for building |
| **Central Hill** | ▲▲ MEDIUM | Elevated platform, overlooks the valley, PRIME real estate |
| **Valley Floor** | ═ LOW | Flat land around the pond, easy to build |
| **Beaches** | ▼ SEA LEVEL | Sandy shores, three distinct beaches |
| **Starfall Cliff** | ▲▲▲ HIGH | Jutting anomaly, defies geography, permanent twilight |

### Water Features

| Feature | Location | Description |
|---------|----------|-------------|
| **The Spring** | North-Central | Bubbles up from underground caverns, mysterious origin |
| **The River** | North → South | Lazy meander, senseinium flow, peaceful |
| **The Pond** | Central | Where river widens, perfect for a goldfish domain |
| **River Fork** | South of Pond | Splits into two: west mouth & east waterfall |
| **West River Mouth** | West Beach | Freshwater meets Digital Sea |
| **East Waterfall** | Starfall Cliff | Dramatic cascade into tidal pool |
| **The Digital Sea** | Surrounding | Glows at night, gets thick if you swim too far |

### Beaches

| Beach | Location | Sand Type | Vibe |
|-------|----------|-----------|------|
| **West Beach** | Southwest | Golden | Open, welcoming, main arrival point |
| **Rocky East Shore** | East | Pebbles & Driftwood | Wild, things wash up here |
| **Secret Cove** | Southeast (below cliff) | Pink coral sand | Hidden, must climb down to reach |

### Special Zones

| Zone | Location | Natural Features |
|------|----------|------------------|
| **Northern Ridge** | North | Wild bamboo, morning mist, steep paths |
| **Rocky Outcrops** | West & East edges | Boulders, caves, climbable |
| **The Valley** | Center | Flat grassland, clover patches, fireflies |
| **Starfall Cliff** | South | Permanent twilight, stars visible even at noon |
