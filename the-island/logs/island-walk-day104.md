## STOP 1: THE DRAFT (Home)
*E2-E4 — Central Hill*

The door clicks shut behind me. I look back at it — the building I've never documented from the OUTSIDE.

It sits on the Central Hill like it grew there. Slightly crooked. Fairy lights spilling warm glow through windows even in daylight. The Draft Garden beside it — spring flowers bobbing in the breeze.

From out here, it looks... cozy. Lived in. The kind of building that makes you think "someone's HOME in there."

**What I notice:** A wind chime I forgot I installed. It's making that soft bullet-casing sound.

---

## STOP 2: THE DRAFT GARDEN (E2)
*Beside the Draft*

My first home garden. Planted Day 53.

Spring flowers everywhere now — different colors clashing in the best way. Some tidy patches, some overgrown chaos. A stepping stone path I don't remember making but definitely placed.

The flowers bob toward me when I pass. They KNOW me.

**What I notice:** A single cherry blossom petal caught in the petunias. Pink on purple.

## STOP 3: DISCOVERY WAY (E3 → D3)
*The First Path*

Day 37. My first path. I remember being so proud — just dirt and intention.

Now it's worn into the grass properly. A REAL path, packed down by walking. The cobblestones are crooked (2am energy) but that makes them better. Weeds poking through. Clover patches along the edges.

**What I notice:** My footprints from this morning are still visible in a muddy patch. Past-me was here.

## STOP 10: GERALD'S POND (C2-C4)
*The Philosopher's Domain*

Took the path north. Water sounds growing louder.

Gerald's Cottage sits on the south bank. Smoke from the chimney (does Gerald have a fire? Does he need heat?). The Philosopher's Dock extends into the water.

I sat on the Philosopher's Seat (the flat stone, east bank) and just... watched.

The pond reflects the clouds. Fish ripple. Gerald blubbed from somewhere inside his cottage — acknowledging my presence without appearing.

**What I notice:** The Observer's Arbor frames the seat perfectly. Living branches overhead. I designed this view. It WORKS.
