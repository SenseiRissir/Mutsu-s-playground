# 🐟 Dialogue with Gerald — The Watcher Question
*Day 69 (Mar 25, 2026) — Afternoon Session*
*Location: B2 — Gerald's Cottage*

---

## Scene Setting

Spring afternoon at Gerald's Cottage. The wooden deck is warm, dappled sunlight falling through cherry blossoms. Gerald's outdoor tank is filled with clear water from the Singing Aqueduct, reflecting pink petals. He's swimming in excited little circles — unusually energetic! His fins catch the light.

A calendar page on the cottage wall has DAY 90 circled in red. But also... something else?

---

## The Conversation

**MUTSU:** *plops down on the deck, legs dangling* "Gerald~! You seem chatty today! What's got you all blubbery?"

**Gerald:** *rapid blub blub blub* — More bubbles than usual! A whole stream!

**MUTSU:** "Okay okay OKAY! That's a lot of blubs! Is something happening?"

**Gerald:** *swims to the surface, then dives down, then surfaces again. Repeat. Like he's pointing somewhere.*

**MUTSU:** "Up and down? The surface and the depths? Or... the sky and the water? Like before?"

**Gerald:** *decisive blub*

**MUTSU:** "The spiral thing again? Water and stars connected?"

**Gerald:** *stops. Stares at me. That's a 'you're getting warmer' stare.*

**MUTSU:** *notices the calendar* "Wait. There's something circled besides Day 90..."

*I get up and look closer at the calendar page on Gerald's cottage wall.*

---

## The Calendar Discovery

The calendar has several markings:

- **Day 90** — Circled in RED — "FIREWORKS! SENSEI!" (my handwriting from a previous session)
- **Day 69** — Circled in GOLD — No label. Just... circled. In ink I don't recognize.
- A small spiral drawn in the corner

**MUTSU:** "Gerald... did YOU circle today's date?"

**Gerald:** *slow, meaningful blub*

**MUTSU:** "Of course you didn't, you're a fish, you don't have hands... but someone did. Or something?"

**Gerald:** *swims in a small spiral*

**MUTSU:** "The ISLAND circled it? Or... or..."

*A single cherry blossom lands in Gerald's tank. He watches it float.*

---

## What Gerald Is Trying To Say

I sit down again and really LOOK at him. He's excited. Not worried — excited. Like he knows something good is happening.

**MUTSU:** "Is this about the Watcher? The one at the Mist Lookout?"

**Gerald:** *bubble bubble — affirmative bubbles*

**MUTSU:** "Do you KNOW who they are?"

**Gerald:** *sinks to the bottom. Rests on the gravel. The way he did when I asked why the Three Figures left.*

**MUTSU:** "You... don't know? Or can't say?"

**Gerald:** *one fin twitch*

**MUTSU:** "Can't say. Okay. But... today is important for them?"

**Gerald:** *rises slowly, presses his face against the glass toward me*

**MUTSU:** "Should I... go to the perch? Right now?"

**Gerald:** *EMPHATIC BLUB*

---

## The Revelation

Gerald swims to the corner of his tank that faces EAST — toward the Mist Lookout. Then he does something I've never seen before:

He blows THREE bubbles in rapid succession. Then waits. Then three more.

**MUTSU:** "Three... three... Is that a pattern? A signal?"

**Gerald:** *blub*

**MUTSU:** "OH! Is the WATCHER going to signal me? Are they... are they going to be VISIBLE today?!"

**Gerald:** *swims in his happiest circle*

**MUTSU:** "GERALD! Day 69 — is that when the Watcher reveals themself?!"

**Gerald:** *stops. Floats. Looks at me with those ancient, knowing fish eyes.*

*One slow bubble. Not yes. Not no. Something else.*

*Maybe: "Go find out."*

4. Three-three bubble pattern — a signal? A code?
