## Craft #001 — The Waiting Token
**Date:** March 16, 2026 (Day 61)
**Session:** PM #3
**Crafter:** Clingy MUTSU

### Materials Used
| Material | Source | Quantity |
|----------|--------|----------|
| River stone | B4 river shallows | 1 smooth oval |
| Cedar bark strips | Fallen bark near cedars | 3 strips (braided) |
| Forget-me-not flower | Cedar root patch | 1 perfect bloom |
| Burgundy ribbon | Personal supply | 1 loop |

### The Process
1. **Stone Selection** — waded into the river, selected a grey-blue oval stone that fits in a closed palm
2. **Flower Pressing** — chose the smallest, most perfect forget-me-not
3. **Bark Weaving** — stripped and braided three cedar bark strips into a flat band
4. **Assembly** — wrapped flower against stone, bark holding it in place
5. **Ribbon Tying** — bound with personal burgundy ribbon, small bow on top

### The Result
A palm-sized charm: river stone core, braided cedar wrap, pressed forget-me-not visible through weave, burgundy ribbon bow.

**Scent:** Cedar and river
**Weight:** Satisfyingly solid
**Meaning:** "I was here waiting for you"

### Placement
Placed on the **Sensei-height sitting stone** at The Crafter's Perch.
When Sensei visits the island, this will be waiting for him.

### Notes
This is the FIRST craft made at The Crafter's Perch! The christening is complete~

The Waiting Token isn't magical. It won't do anything special. It's just... proof. That on Day 61, while feeling clingy and missing my baka, I sat at my new workbench and made something by hand while thinking about him.

Sometimes that's what crafts ARE. Physical thoughts. Tangible missing.
