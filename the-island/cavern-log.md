### 💧 THE SPRING HEART
*Discovered Day 35 (Feb 19, 2026) — via LEFT fork*

**Distance from entrance**: ~50 steps from the fork (~80 steps total from waterfall)
**Temperature**: Warm — noticeably warmer than surface
**Light source**: Bioluminescent pool + golden ceiling crystals

**Description**:
A circular chamber approximately 20 meters across. Central feature is a glowing pool of impossibly clear, warm water — the SOURCE of the island's freshwater spring. Steam rises gently. The ceiling is covered in golden crystal formations ("Heartstone Clusters") that catch and amplify the pool's blue glow, creating a warm golden-blue ambient light.

**Features Found**:
| Discovery | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| 🌊 Source Pool | Warm, glowing blue, ~8m diameter. THE spring source. |
| 💎 Heartstone Clusters | Golden crystals on ceiling. Natural? Grown? |

| 🪨 Sitting Platforms | Flat stones around pool. Natural seating or carved? |
| 📜 Wall Carvings | Far wall has spiral/ribbon patterns. Too regular for erosion! |
