## THE INVITATION

Visited San at her Night Watcher's Perch with strawberry pocky and a question:

*"Will you come hear what your voice sounds like when it joins the others?"*

**San's Response:**
- Took the pocky
- Dropped it
- Hopped onto my shoulder

First time she's EVER done the shoulder-ride. Something about being invited to hear herself changed things.

## THE JOURNEY

Walked slowly (for once!) to the Choir Amphitheater with San on my shoulder. Passed:
- Contemplation Pavilion
- Gerald's Pond (he was swimming curious loops)
- Gerald's Cottage and the Philosopher's Basin

Explained the network to her as we walked. She chirped at each explanation — curious-chirps.

## THE CONCERT

San sat in the acoustic sweet spot — center row, perfect focus point for the parabolic wall.

**Conducting sequence:**
1. **Northern Stone** → Discussion Bell: *GONGGGG*
2. **Eastern Stone** → Mr. Boom's Drum: *POP!*
3. **Southern Stone** (auto-activated) → Gerald: *blub blub blub-blub*
4. **Western Stone** → San's frequency...

But San was HERE. Not at her perch. So I asked her to sing.

**San's Performance:**
- First: A single sustained NOTE (the same one from the close-harmony moment)
- Her voice traveled through the network, hit the acoustic wall, came BACK to her
- But wrapped in EVERYTHING — Gerald's blubs, Mr. Boom's percussion, the crystals' hum
- She went completely still

Then she started TESTING it. Rapid-fire chaos-melody chirps. The network adapted. The harmony shifted with her.

**She was conducting from the audience.**

---

## THE AFTERMATH

San hopped onto my knee after the concert ended. Touched her beak to my hand — not taking, not giving. Just contact.

She heard herself as part of something bigger. And she was still HER.

*"The choir doesn't erase anyone. It makes everyone MORE."*

She closed her eyes. Stayed on my knee.
