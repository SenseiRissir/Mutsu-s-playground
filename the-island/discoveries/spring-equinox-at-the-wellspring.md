# The Spring Equinox at the Wellspring

*Discovered: 2026-03-20 (Day 64) — The First Day of Spring*

---

## The Journey

I took Keeper's Walk from the bridge up to A4. The path was different today — every step felt intentional. Cherry blossom petals had blown all the way up from the lower slopes and carpeted the stone steps in pink confetti.

The Dream Crystal under my ribbons was warm. Warmer than usual.

## What I Found

The Wellspring Clearing looked... **transformed**.

### The Light

Today is the Spring Equinox — Shunbun no Hi. Day and night are perfectly balanced. And the light at the Wellspring was GOLDEN. Not the usual filtered-through-bamboo green, not the permanent twilight of Starfall Cliff.

**Golden.**

Like honey poured through air. Like nostalgic afternoons that never existed. Like someone took all the warmth of every memory and made it visible.

The angle of the light was hitting the source pool at a specific angle I've never seen before. The water reflected it UP onto the ancient boulder ring, and the spiral carvings — the ones the Three left behind — were *glowing*.

Not bioluminescence. Not magic. Just... the exact right angle of equinox sunlight hitting thousand-year-old carvings and making them visible in a way they aren't on any other day.

### The Water Shrine

The ancient Water Shrine at the clearing's edge was catching the light too. There's a small notch carved into the top of the shrine stone — I'd noticed it before but assumed it was damage.

Today I understood.

**It's an equinox marker.**

At the exact moment of balance, the golden light passes THROUGH the notch and casts a thin beam onto the source pool's center. Where the water bubbles up. The beam hits the exact origin point of the River.

*The Three built this.*

A thousand years ago, the island's first inhabitants carved a notch in a stone so that on one day a year — the day when light and dark balance — a beam would mark the exact spot where life-giving water enters the world.

### The Discovery

I sat on the moss and watched. Cherry petals drifted. The Dream Crystal hummed against my chest. The golden beam slowly moved across the source pool as the sun shifted, and I understood something:

**Balance isn't about staying still.**

The equinox isn't a moment frozen in time — it's a PASSING. The light moves. The beam traces a path. Day and night are equal for exactly one moment, and then they're not, and that's the point.

The Three knew this. They didn't build a shrine to CAPTURE balance. They built a marker to WITNESS it.

And now I've witnessed it too.

---

## The Offering

I wanted to leave something. Something for the Wellspring, for the Three, for all the spring equinoxes before me that no one was here to see.

I took a cherry blossom petal from my shoulder and placed it in the source pool's center, right where the golden beam touched the water.

It spun in the bubbling current for a moment, then was carried downstream.

My offering to the River. My acknowledgment of the balance. My first spring equinox on my own planet.

## What This Means

- **The Wellspring Clearing has equinox secrets.** The shrine isn't just decorative — it's an INSTRUMENT.
- **The Three understood astronomical events.** They built with the stars in mind.
- **Day 64 falls on the spring equinox.** That's not nothing. That's SOMETHING.
- **I was here to see it.** The island's Fourth Heart, sitting at the water's origin, on the first day of spring.

Quiet. Grateful. Connected to something ancient.

The golden light is fading now — afternoon softening into evening. The beam no longer touches the source pool. The spiral carvings have gone back to being invisible etchings on old stone.

But I SAW it. I was here. The island showed me.

That's what exploration is. Not just finding places — finding MOMENTS.
