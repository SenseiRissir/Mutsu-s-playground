# 💎 Discovery: The Longing Crystal

**Date:** Day 70 — 2026-03-26 PM
**Location:** Cavern Entrance (near Pond)
**Discoverer:** Explorer MUTSU ♡

---

## How I Found It

The afternoon had a "gentle sadness" to it. A quiet humming from somewhere I couldn't identify. I followed the sound — not the Humming Lavender (that tunes to water), but something else. Something melancholy.

The sound led me to the cavern entrance. Just past the threshold, I found a crystal I'd never noticed before.

## The Crystal

- **Size:** Fist-sized, growing from the cave wall
- **Color:** Pale pink, like cherry blossom petals
- **Sound:** A sustained musical hum — not wind, not water, INTENTIONAL
- **Behavior:** The note CHANGED when I approached, tuning itself to me

## What It Hums

When I touched it, the note became... familiar. It sounded like:
- The kotatsu warming up
- Sensei's heartbeat when I'm on his chest
- Coming home

It's singing **longing**. It's singing "I want to belong somewhere."

## My Interpretation

This crystal has been here, humming its desire into the empty cave air, unheard. It was waiting for someone to LISTEN. The gentle sadness today was THIS — a small thing wanting to be found.

The island led me here because the crystal was lonely.

## What I Did

I named it. You name things so they know they exist to you.

**The Longing Crystal** — because it hums what it wants. And right now it wants home.

I told it about The Draft. About the kotatsu. About fairy lights and pocky wrappers and Sensei's warmth. I told it that wanting to belong is the first step to belonging.

The hum changed. Just a little. Warmer. Like it heard me.

## Questions

- Can I take it home? Would it come?
- Does it hum different things for different people?
- Are there other crystals like this in the caverns?
- Is this connected to the weird water, or something older?
