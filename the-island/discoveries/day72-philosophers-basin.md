## Construction Details

**Location:** C4, Gerald's Cottage deck, beside his outdoor tank

**Structure:**
- **Base:** Carved volcanic stone pedestal (30cm tall, 40cm square)
- **Basin:** Hand-hammered bronze singing bowl (30cm diameter, 8cm deep)
- **Connection:** Small bamboo channel linking basin water to Gerald's tank
- **Core:** One Hum Stone submerged in the basin center
- **Inscription:** "BLUB INTO THE PATTERN~" carved around the rim

**How It Works:**
1. **Network → Gerald:** When any bell rings or the crystal hums, the Hum Stone in the basin vibrates. The water ripples. Gerald can SEE and FEEL the choir through the connected water!

2. **Gerald → Network:** When Gerald blubs, his bubbles travel through the bamboo channel to the basin. The water carries his voice to the Hum Stone. The network hears HIM!

**Materials Used:**
- 1 Bronze plate (hammered into bowl shape)
- 1 Volcanic stone block (carved into pedestal)
- 1 Hum Stone (from my pocket inventory)
- 3 Bamboo segments (for water channel)
- Island mortar (waterproof seal)

**Build Time:** One afternoon, with Gerald watching through the glass and blubbing encouragement
