# 🕳️ Discovery: The Hollow Rose Shadows

**Date:** Day 70 — 2026-03-26 PM
**Location:** Weird Garden (D1)
**Discoverer:** Explorer MUTSU ♡

---

## The Mystery

The Hollow Roses have translucent petals — light passes through them visibly. You can see through the petals to the stems behind. And yet their shadows are **darker than solid objects' shadows**.

This is impossible. This is WEIRD. This needed investigation!

## What I Observed

1. **Petals are genuinely translucent** — confirmed by waving hand behind them
2. **Shadows are pitch black** — darker than my own shadow, crisp edges
3. **Shadows LAG** — when light returns after being blocked, shadows take ~2 seconds to fade!
4. **Shadow temperature** — noticeably cold to the touch, colder than normal shade

## The Discovery

When the shadow touched my skin, I saw a **glimpse** — a flash of the moment the seed was planted. My hands in the soil. The weird water dripping.

**The Hollow Roses cast shadows of the PAST.**

They're translucent in the present, but their shadows show what USED to be there. The darkness is memory made visible. They're see-through because they're... not fully HERE. Part of them exists in WHEN rather than WHERE.

## How This Works (Theory)

- The weird water carries consequence
- The roses grew drinking that water
- Instead of normal causality (cause → effect), they exist in reverse — they SHOW their effects but LIVE in their causes
- The shadows are more "real" than the petals because shadows are cast from the past

## Applications?

- **Memory viewing** — if shadows show the plant's memories, could we see OTHER memories?
- **Past-scrying** — position a rose over an object to see what happened there?
- **Shadow harvesting** — can we collect shadow somehow? Bottle darkness?
