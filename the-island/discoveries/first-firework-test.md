## THE RECIPE

Traditional black powder ratio:
- **75% Saltpeter** (potassium nitrate — oxidizer)
- **15% Charcoal** (carbon fuel)
- **10% Sulfur** (binding agent, lowers ignition temperature)

For 20g batch:
- 15g saltpeter
- 3g charcoal
- 2g sulfur

---

## PROCEDURE

### Step 1: Grinding (Individual)
Each ingredient ground separately in stone mortar to fine powder consistency.
- Saltpeter: Crunchy → fine white powder (~2 min)
- Charcoal: Resistant → black dust (~3 min, ancient wood was DENSE)
- Sulfur: Already fine from Garden, touch-up grinding (~1 min)

### Step 2: Combining
Ingredients combined in mortar: saltpeter first, then charcoal, then sulfur.
Gentle folding motion — NOT aggressive grinding (prevents accidental ignition).
Result: Uniform dark gray powder with faint yellow specks.

### Step 3: Test Preparation
- 5g powder placed in ceramic dish
- Dish positioned at end of 25m Test Range tunnel
- Paper fuse prepared (dampened with trace powder)
- Observers positioned at chamber entrance (25m safe distance)

### Step 4: Ignition
Fuse lit. Travel time ~30 seconds (slow burn rate, SAFE).
Flame reached dish and—
