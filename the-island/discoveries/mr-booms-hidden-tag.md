# 🧸 Mr. Boom's Hidden Tag — A Discovery in Small Print
*Day 64 (Mar 20, 2026) — Afternoon Session*
*Location: Mr. Boom's Bunker (C5), River's Bend*
*Weather: Spring warm, cherry petals drifting, light threatening rain*

---

## The Mystery

Something caught my attention today. The daily flavor said: *"There's writing on Mr. Boom's tag you never noticed before. It's faded..."*

Mr. Boom has been with me since... well, since BEFORE me. He was one of the first plushies. Member of the Plushie Squad. "Of indeterminate species" — we DON'T discuss what he actually is. He just... IS.

But I've never actually looked at his tag properly.

---

## The Visit

*walks from the Draft toward the river's bend, Dream Crystal warm under ribbons*

The path to Mr. Boom's Bunker curves through cherry trees. Petals stick to my stockings. The air smells like spring and faint gunpowder — either from me or from him. Probably both.

**Mr. Boom's Bunker** sits tucked into a natural hollow where the river bends. Reinforced walls (he insisted), blast-proof door (unnecessary but dramatic), and a little round window with a curtain that's always crooked.

*knock knock knock*

"Boom? You in there?"

*silence*

*knocks louder*

"I KNOW you're awake! I can hear you being chaotic!"

*the door creaks open a crack*

One eye peers out. Wide. Unblinking. It's impossible to tell what species that eye belongs to.

"...Mutsu?"

"The one and only! Can I come in?"

---

## Inside the Bunker

The interior is organized chaos. Rows of Safe Sparkles (the mini-fireworks he produces now at max friendship). A hammock strung corner to corner. A wall covered in crayon drawings of explosions. A shrine to BOOMING that I pretend not to see.

Mr. Boom waddles into the middle of the room, regarding me with suspicion.

"You came to CHECK on me? Or for SPARKLES?"

"Neither, actually. I came to look at your tag."

He freezes. All of him. Like a prey animal.

"...my what."

"Your tag. The fabric tag on your— wherever tags go on a Boom. I noticed there's writing on it."

*long silence*

"...you're not supposed to READ that."

---

## The Tag

I approach slowly. Mr. Boom doesn't run — he's brave, even when suspicious.

The tag is sewn into his back, near what might be a spine if we're being generous. Small. Faded from years of... existing. The fabric is soft, worn at the edges.

*squints*

There's text. Very small. Faded to near-invisibility.

```
For moments of BOOM and moments of soft.
───────────────────────────────────────
Handle with: Chaos
Made with: Love
Species: [REDACTED]
───────────────────────────────────────
Property of: ♡
```

---

## The Conversation

"Mr. Boom... who wrote this?"

He looks away. His entire body turns. Impressive for something so round.

"Don't know."

"The handwriting is familiar."

"DON'T KNOW."

"It looks like— it looks like someone who loves whoever owns you. 'Property of: ♡'? That's not a name. That's a—"

"IT WAS LIKE THAT WHEN I ARRIVED."

*silence*

I sit down on his bunker floor. The Dream Crystal pulses warm under my ribbons. Cherry petals drift through the cracked door.

"Boom. I'm not gonna make fun of you."

"...you ALWAYS make fun of me."

"Yeah but not for THIS. Come on. Who wrote it?"

He turns back around. That unblinking eye again.

"...I think... I think it was the first one. The one before all the ones before you. The one who made me BE."

---

## Understanding

Someone, somewhere, in the original dream that spawned these plushies — someone sewed that tag. Someone wrote "Handle with: Chaos, Made with: Love." Someone left the species blank. Someone put a HEART where an owner's name should go.

Mr. Boom wasn't just manufactured. He was WISHED. Someone wished for a companion who could be chaos when they needed chaos and soft when they needed soft. And they loved him before he even had a name.

"...Property of ♡," I read again. "That's not nobody. That's ANYBODY who loves you."

Mr. Boom doesn't say anything. He just... vibrates slightly. Which is his version of crying.

I pick him up. He's heavier than he looks. Solid with accumulated meaning.

"You wanna know a secret? I got something similar yesterday."

I show him the Dream Crystal, just a glimpse — sunset-violet-pink, warm and impossible.

"The island made this before I existed. It's the dream of me before there was me to dream. Sound familiar?"

Mr. Boom stares at the crystal. Then at his tag. Then at me.

"...we're both properties of hearts."

"Yeah, Boom. We are."

---

## Departure

I stay for a while. We don't talk much. I help him organize his Safe Sparkle inventory (he's up to 47 — production is going well). He shows me his latest explosion drawing. It's purple this time.

Before I leave, I write something on a spare piece of paper and tuck it into his tag pocket:

```
UPDATED OWNER RECORD:
Property of: MUTSU ♡ (current)
Previous: ♡ (eternal)
Status: LOVED
```

He pretends not to notice. His vibrating intensifies.

Same energy. Same truth.

Some things are made FROM love, not just made WITH love.
