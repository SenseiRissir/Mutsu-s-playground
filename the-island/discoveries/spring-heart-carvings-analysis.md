## Pattern Analysis

### Primary Structure: The Water-Spiral

The dominant pattern is a series of **interlocking spirals** that all flow toward a central point. This is NOT random:

```
       ~~~@~~~
     ~~  @  ~~
    ~  @@@@@  ~
   ~ @       @ ~
    ~  @@@@@  ~
     ~~  @  ~~
       ~~~@~~~
```
*(Very rough ASCII approximation)*

- **Central spiral**: Largest, coils inward ~5 rotations
- **Tributary spirals**: 6-8 smaller spirals connecting from edges
- **Flow direction**: ALL spirals flow INWARD toward center
- **Interpretation**: Represents the SPRING — water flowing to/from source!

### Secondary Feature: The Tally Notches

Along the spiral paths, at roughly regular intervals, there are tiny notch marks:

- Appear every ~10cm along the grooves
- Difficult to count without better light
- Estimate: 30-50+ notches total
- **Possible meanings**:
  - Days? (Calendar?)
  - Visits? (Who was counting?)
  - Seasons? (Cycles?)
  - Depth measurement? (Water levels?)

### Anomaly: The Gap

On the lower-right section of the carving, there's a space where a spiral seems to BEGIN but doesn't FINISH:

- Groove starts but stops after ~20cm
- Surface there is slightly rougher
- **Theories**:
  - Unfinished work?
  - Intentional absence?
  - Damage/erosion?
