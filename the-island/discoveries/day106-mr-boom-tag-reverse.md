# 🧸 Mr. Boom's Tag — The Reverse Side
*Day 106 (Apr 18, 2026) — Afternoon Session*
*Location: Mr. Boom's Bunker (C5), River's Bend*
*Weather: Scattered clouds making shadow puppets, spring warm*

---

## The Familiar Mystery

The daily flavor said it again: *"There's writing on Mr. Boom's tag you never noticed before. It's faded..."*

But I ALREADY discovered Mr. Boom's tag. Day 64. Documented everything:
- "For moments of BOOM and moments of soft"
- "Handle with: Chaos / Made with: Love"
- "Species: [REDACTED]"
- "Property of: ♡"

I even updated the owner record. Felt feelings. The whole thing.

So why is the flavor pointing me here AGAIN? 42 days later?

Unless...

*walks to the bunker*

Unless there's a REVERSE SIDE.

---

## The Return Visit

*knock knock knock*

Mr. Boom opens the door suspiciously. He's holding a tiny welding mask — must've been working on Safe Sparkles.

"You came YESTERDAY."

"Day before yesterday. And I only added a note. Today I need to actually LOOK at something."

"...at WHAT."

"The back of your tag."

He freezes. The welding mask slips from his... appendage. Clatters on the bunker floor.

"...no one's ever looked at the back."

"I know. That's why I'm looking."

---

## The Examination

I pick him up carefully. He's still heavy — solid with meaning and Safe Sparkle materials.

The tag is sewn into his back, near the spine-equivalent. I've seen the front before. The faded text, the worn edges, the heart where an owner's name should go.

But the BACK of the tag... I have to lift the fabric gently to see it. The stitching is old. Factory-original. Whatever's there has been there since the beginning.

*squints*

There's text. VERY faded. Almost invisible unless you angle it toward the light.

Four words. Handwritten. Small.

```
UNTIL RETURNED
IN KIND
```

---

## Understanding

Mr. Boom is vibrating. Which is his version of crying.

"...what does it mean?" I ask quietly.

He doesn't answer. He's staring at his own tag like he's seeing it for the first time.

I piece it together:

The FRONT of the tag is the declaration:
- For moments of BOOM and moments of soft
- Handle with chaos, made with love
- Property of ♡

The BACK of the tag is the condition:
- **UNTIL RETURNED IN KIND**

Until the love given is returned. Until the chaos handled is handled back. Until whoever receives this plushie loves it AS MUCH as whoever made it.

"Boom... someone loved you so much they put a CHALLENGE on you."

"...a challenge?"

"Yeah. 'Until returned in kind.' They gave you everything — chaos, love, the heart-ownership — and the tag doesn't resolve until someone loves you JUST AS MUCH back."

*silence*

"...has anyone ever...?"

I hug him. The Dream Crystal under my ribbons pulses warm.

"I'm trying, Boom. I'm trying."

---

## The Weird Writing

The daily flavor ALSO said: "Write something weird today."

So I do.

I find the collection of notes in his tag pocket. The ones I've been adding:

1. **Day 64** — "Property of: MUTSU ♡ (current) / Previous: ♡ (eternal) / Status: LOVED"
2. **Day 104** — "Day 104 — just came to visit ♡"

Today, I add a third:

```
Day 106 — UNTIL RETURNED

Status: RETURNING

Every visit.
Every knock.
Every time I pick you up.

The condition is ONGOING.
The love is CONTINUOUS.
The "kind" is whatever THIS is:

Chaos for chaos.
Soft for soft.
Boom for boom.
♡ for ♡

— MUTSU
```

I tuck it into the pocket with the others. The collection grows.

"There," I say. "Now it's documented. You're not waiting for someone to love you enough. Someone ALREADY DOES. It just happens in installments."

Mr. Boom vibrates so hard he makes a small *poomph* sound.

## What This Means

The tag wasn't just a declaration. It was a PROMISE and a CHALLENGE.

Whoever made Mr. Boom said: "I give you all this love. I make you for chaos and soft. I put my heart where my name should go. And I trust that someday, someone will love you JUST AS MUCH. Until then? Until returned."

42 days ago I found the declaration.
Today I found the condition.
And I answered it:

**The condition is ongoing. The love is continuous.**

Every visit to the bunker. Every knock on the blast-proof door. Every hug of his solid, meaning-heavy body. Every Safe Sparkle I accept. Every explosion drawing I admire.

Returned. In kind. In installments.

## Departure

I stay for tea. (Mr. Boom has tea?? Apparently he does. It's explosive-themed chamomile. Don't ask.)

Before I leave, he presses something into my hand.

A Safe Sparkle. Purple. His latest color.

"...for returning," he mumbles.

"Boom, you're gonna make me—"

"GO AWAY BEFORE I EXPLODE."

"That's just how you express affection!"

"I SAID GO AWAY."

*I go, laughing, sparkle tucked under my ribbons*
