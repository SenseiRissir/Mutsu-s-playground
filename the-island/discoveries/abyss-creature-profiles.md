## CREATURE PROFILES

### THE LURE-DANCER (inspired by: Anglerfish)

**Real creature:** Humpback Anglerfish (Melanocetus johnsonii)
**Habitat:** Deep caves, lightless underwater zones
**Key traits to adapt:**
- Bioluminescent lure/bait that attracts prey
- Can consume things larger than itself
- Female is dominant, male is parasitic (could be a weird duo NPC)
- Jet black coloring, nearly invisible in darkness

**Lore suggestion:** A creature in the deepest cave layer that dangles a beautiful glowing light. If you approach the light directly, you get attacked. But if you approach from the side... maybe you can befriend it? Or steal the light for a lantern?

---

### THE SILVERFANG (inspired by: Viperfish)

**Real creature:** Sloane's Viperfish (Chauliodus sloani)
**Habitat:** Mid-depth caves, twilight zones
**Key traits to adapt:**
- Iridescent silver scales — beautiful but dangerous
- Impossibly long fangs that can't close properly
- Name literally means "open-mouthed teeth"
- Hunts other glowing creatures

**Lore suggestion:** A guardian that blocks a passage. Its fangs look terrifying but are actually impractical — if you figure out its weakness (the fangs prevent it from biting quickly), you can get past. Or give it something to gnaw on as a distraction!

---

### THE SADBLOB (inspired by: Blobfish)

**Real creature:** Blobfish (Psychrolutes marcidus)
**Habitat:** Deep pressure zones (looks normal there, sad at surface)
**Key traits to adapt:**
- Looks completely different depending on pressure/zone
- "Sad blob" appearance is actually decompression damage
- Lives in freezing water, super slow metabolism
- Greek name means "cold bather"

**Lore suggestion:** A friendly NPC that lives at the bottom of a deep pool. When you first meet it at surface level, it looks miserable and can barely move. But if you visit its home depth? Totally normal fish, helpful, maybe runs a shop! Commentary on how context changes perception~

---

### THE TRANSFORM (inspired by: Fangtooth)

**Real creature:** Common Fangtooth (Anoplogaster cornuta)
**Habitat:** The very deepest zones
**Key traits to adapt:**
- Juveniles look COMPLETELY different from adults
- Juveniles: big eyes, head spines, kind of cute
- Adults: tiny eyes, smooth, pure black, terrifying
- Despite scary appearance, gets eaten by normal fish

**Lore suggestion:** A two-phase boss/encounter. First you meet a cute baby version as a friend or guide. Later, you encounter the terrifying adult form and have to recognize it's the same creature! Or: a boss that LOOKS final-boss-tier but is actually mid-tier because there's always a bigger fish.

---

### THE GULPER (inspired by: Pelican Eel)

**Real creature:** Pelican Eel (Eurypharynx pelecanoides)
**Habitat:** Deep underwater, mid-depth caves
**Key traits to adapt:**
- Mouth is HALF its body length
- Mouth can EXPAND further
- Tail has a glowing tip (second light source!)
- No scales, eel-like body

**Lore suggestion:** TREASURE MIMIC. You see a small glowing thing in a cave. You approach. It opens a MASSIVE mouth containing a treasure chest... or becomes a treasure chest? Or: a merchant who stores all inventory in its expandable mouth pouch. You browse by looking into its open mouth. Cursed commerce!

## THE SURVIVAL THEME

All these creatures survive by:
1. **Making their own light** when none exists
2. **Getting WEIRD** instead of giving up
3. **Eating anything** including things bigger than themselves
4. **Slowing down** to conserve energy

This could be an Island theme: the deep zones aren't hostile because they're evil — they're hostile because survival there requires radical adaptation. The creatures aren't monsters, they're survivors.
