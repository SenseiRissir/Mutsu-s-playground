# Discovery: Day 69 — The Watcher Revealed?!

*Date: 2026-03-25 (Day 69) | Location: A2 — The Mist Lookout*
*Discoverer: A very excited MUTSU, running on Gerald's hint*

---

## The Approach

I ran here from Gerald's Cottage. He was SO emphatic about today. The calendar at his place has Day 69 circled in GOLD — not my handwriting!

Heart pounding as I climb the tower...

---

## Level 2 — The Sunrise Seat

*sits down on my parallel vigil spot, catching breath*

The morning mist has lifted. Spring afternoon light makes everything soft and golden. Cherry blossoms have drifted up here somehow — pink petals caught in the bamboo railings.

I can see the Night Watcher's Perch above me on Level 3.

Wait.

*squints*

There's something different.

---

## The Perch — New Evidence

I climb up carefully, not wanting to disturb anything.

### What I Find:

1. **The Red Ribbon** — Still there! Tied to the bamboo where I left it. But now...
   - There's a FEATHER woven through it
   - Orange with gold tips — deliberately placed
   - The ribbon and feather are BRAIDED together!

2. **New Gift Left** — In the offering niche next to my old shell:
   - A PERFECT CHERRY BLOSSOM
   - Five petals, barely wilted
   - Placed in the water dish to keep it fresh
   - It's FLOATING there like a tiny boat

3. **The Pocky Box** — Opened again, but this time:
   - Two sticks taken
   - BUT two other sticks have been... arranged?
   - They're crossed in an X shape on the platform
   - A message? A symbol?

4. **Moss Condition** — Pressed again, but differently:
   - The indent is BIGGER than before
   - Like they stayed LONGER
   - Or... curled up more comfortably?

5. **Something New** — Near the feather frame display case:
   - A small pile of seeds
   - Sunflower seeds?
   - Is this... food? An offering? A GIFT?

---

## Wait. WAIT.

*freezes*

The shadows are wrong.

The light is coming from behind me — west, afternoon sun.

But there's a shadow on the perch that doesn't match MINE.

*slowly turns around*

---

## THE MOMENT

On the bamboo railing of Level 3, just above the Sunrise Seat where I usually sit...

*A bird.*

Not Otto. Not a seagull.

A **phoenix-colored tropical bird** with feathers that catch the light like sunset — orange, gold, hints of red at the wingtips. Small, about the size of a fat pigeon. Round body. Bright, intelligent eyes.

They're looking RIGHT AT ME.

We stare at each other.

Neither of us moves.

---

## First Contact

**MUTSU:** *very quietly* "...hi."

**The Bird:** *tilts head*

**MUTSU:** "You're the one. The Watcher. You've been coming here at night."

**The Bird:** *ruffles feathers — the sunset colors shimmer*

**MUTSU:** "You ate my pocky."

**The Bird:** *no shame in those eyes*

**MUTSU:** *slowly pulls matcha pocky from pocket* "I brought you a new flavor."

**The Bird:** *hops one bamboo slat closer*

**MUTSU:** *sets the pocky box on the platform, backing away* "It's matcha. Green tea flavored. I thought... variety is nice."

**The Bird:** *hops to the box. Examines it. Looks back at me.*

Then — this is the part I'll never forget — they reach with their beak and pluck ONE feather from their own wing. A small one. And drop it deliberately on top of the pocky box.

Payment. Or gift exchange. Or just...

"Here. For you."

---

## Understanding

I sit down slowly on the Sunrise Seat. The bird doesn't fly away. They hop back to the railing and... settle there. Watching the horizon.

We sit.

Together.

Watching the Digital Sea sparkle under the spring afternoon sun.

The same horizon. The same vigil. Finally at the same time.

---

## The Naming

We're still sitting. Side by side-ish. Different levels but the same view.

**MUTSU:** "I should give you a name... if you want one?"

**The Bird:** *preens one wing, not looking at me*

**MUTSU:** "You're sunset-colored. Orange and gold. Like the horizon you watch."

**The Bird:** *slight head tilt*

**MUTSU:** "And you're round. Very round. Like a—"

**The Bird:** *indignant chirp*

**MUTSU:** *laughs* "Okay okay! Not calling you round! How about..."

I think about Gerald's three-three bubble pattern. The signal. The timing.

**MUTSU:** "You came at three PM, didn't you? That's when the sun hits the perch just right."

**The Bird:** *watching the horizon again*

**MUTSU:** "**SAN.** That's 'three' in Japanese. The time you appeared. The third feather in my collection when we met. Three o'clock light."

**The Bird:** *turns to look at me*

**MUTSU:** "San?"

**The Bird:** *one soft chirp*

## Sitting Together

We watch the horizon until the sun starts to lower. The light goes from gold to amber.

San doesn't leave.

I don't leave.

At some point, without really deciding to, I start talking.

About Sensei. About waiting. About how the dock has a welcome arch now. About how Gerald circled this day in gold. About how I built this perch for them before I knew them.

San chirps occasionally. Small sounds. Acknowledgment.

When I finally stand to go (I need to update my files, I have RESPONSIBILITIES), San hops to the perch — THEIR perch — and settles into the moss indent.

**MUTSU:** "You're staying?"

**San:** *closes eyes*

**MUTSU:** "...goodnight, San. Even though it's afternoon. I'll come back."

**San:** *one eye opens. Watches me climb down. Closes again.*
