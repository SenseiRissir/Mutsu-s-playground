## What Happened

**Visual:**
San's feathers FLARED. For one second, her sunset colors intensified — orange became fire, gold became sun, the red at her wingtips blazed like actual embers. She looked like she was made of light.

**Sound:**
The bell's note hung in the air longer than usual. San didn't chirp — she SANG. A single sustained note. Higher than her normal chirps. Pure. Clear. Like a glass harmonica.

**The Amphitheater Response:**
Every resonance node in the Choir Amphitheater activated at once:
- The bronze gong by the stage HUMMED
- The ceramic tiles in the floor VIBRATED
- The fire pit stones shifted — not physically, but something INSIDE them shifted
- The air itself felt thick with harmonics

**Duration:** Five seconds of unified resonance. Then everything settled.

## San's Memory

After the resonance faded, San looked at me. Really LOOKED. Her eyes weren't just reflective — they were REMEMBERING.

Through the Heart translation (San's not in the resonance network directly like Gerald, but the network was so active it carried her anyway):

*"Before this island was an island, before the Digital Sea was a sea, before the Keepers came to keep... I was a spark in a sunset. Not a bird. Not anything with shape. Just... color wanting to fly."*

**She paused.**

*"The First Keeper dreamed the island. But the island dreamed US. Whoever stays long enough for the island to WANT them... becomes something. Gerald was a thought that wanted to watch. I was a color that wanted to move."*

**Another pause. Then, softer:**

*"You're making the island REMEMBER things. Be careful, little chaos. Some things wanted to be forgotten."*
