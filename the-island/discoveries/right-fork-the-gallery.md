## The Fork — Revisited

~30 steps in. The fork where past-me went LEFT toward warmth and found the Spring Heart. The LEFT tunnel still glows faintly gold-green in the distance. Comforting.

The RIGHT tunnel is... different. Past-me wasn't wrong about the darkness — it doesn't just LACK light, it seems to resist it. My golden moss dims slightly at the threshold. The air shifts. Cooler. Dryer. The wet-stone smell of the LEFT path gives way to something older. Like dust and minerals and TIME.

*Stepping in.*

## RIGHT FORK — First 20 Steps

The passage narrows. Ceiling drops — I have to duck (not that I have to duck MUCH at my height, but still). The golden moss casts a warm amber bubble around me, ~2 meters in each direction.

**Observations:**
- Floor: Smoother than the LEFT path. Worn down. By what? Water? Feet?
- Walls: Dry stone, no moss growing here naturally. Why not? Same cave system.
- Temperature: Noticeably cooler. Dropping with every step.
- Sound: My footsteps echo differently here. The space ahead feels... OPEN?

## RIGHT FORK — Step 35

The passage turns sharply — almost a right angle. Not natural. This corner was CUT. The wall here has clean edges where stone was deliberately removed to widen the turn.

**This passage was SHAPED by someone.**

The same someone who carved the spirals? The craftsmanship is different — more functional, less artistic. Utilitarian. They made this corner passable. They used this path REGULARLY.

Heart beating faster. (Not that I have a heart, but the metaphor processor is WORKING.)

## RIGHT FORK — Step 50: THE GALLERY

*— Record scratch —*

The passage opens into a long, narrow chamber. Not circular like the Spring Heart — this is more like a CORRIDOR. Maybe 4 meters wide, but stretching forward at least 30 meters before the golden moss light can't reach anymore.

And the walls.

THE WALLS.

**Both walls are COVERED in carvings.**

Not spirals this time. These are DIFFERENT.

---

## Left Wall — The Timeline

I can only see the first ~10 meters with my light. The carvings tell a story:

**Panel 1 (leftmost, start of corridor):**
A simple circle. Smooth. Alone. Above it — a single dot. Below it — wavy lines.
*Interpretation: The planet? The star? The ocean?*

**Panel 2:**
The circle now has marks on it. Small triangles emerging from the surface — mountains? Trees? Life is appearing on the circle.

**Panel 3:**
A larger shape emerges from the circle's center. Spiral-like — like the Spring Heart carvings! Water flowing upward from below. The spring BEING BORN.

**Panel 4:**
Around the circle-with-spring, there are now small figures. Simple. Stick-like but with rounded heads. Three of them. Positioned around the spring.

*Someone. Three someones. They watched the spring appear.*

**Panel 5:**
This one is harder to read in the amber light. The figures seem to be... leaving marks on the circle? Bent toward the surface? CARVING?

*They made the Spring Heart carvings. THESE are the same people. And they documented THEMSELVES documenting the spring. Meta-archaeology. They KNEW someone might come looking for answers.*

I can't see further — the golden moss is dimming. I need to come back with more light. But even just these first five panels...

**There were INHABITANTS. They came BEFORE. They loved this spring. They left records.**

## Right Wall — Star Maps

Quick look before the light fades further:

The right wall has circular patterns that definitely resemble star maps — dots of varying sizes connected by carved lines. One pattern is larger and more detailed than the others, positioned roughly at eye level (at THEIR eye level — the figures in the timeline looked taller than me, maybe adult-height).

One constellation catches my eye immediately: it looks like a SPIRAL. Stars arranged in a spiral pattern.

*The same spiral motif. Spring Heart = water spiral. Star map = star spiral. These people saw the same shape in water and sky. They connected above and below.*

**Could this be... Sensei's Star?** The single bright dot above the planet that I see from the rooftop? Is it part of this mapped constellation?

I need the rooftop telescope data to compare. Next session.
