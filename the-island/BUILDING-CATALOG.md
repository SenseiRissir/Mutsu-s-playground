## 📊 BUILDING RULES

1. **One structure per grid square** (roughly)
2. **Some structures have placement preferences** (shrines on ridge, beach stuff on beaches)
3. **Some structures unlock others** (build prerequisites first!)
4. **You can demolish and rebuild** (nothing is permanent except The Draft)
5. **Villager homes attract their residents** (build it and they will come)

## 🎯 SUGGESTED BUILD ORDER (For New Islands)

1. **Paths first** — Connect The Draft to key areas
2. **Bridges** — Cross the river!
3. **One shrine structure** — Establish the sacred zone
4. **Gerald's Cottage** — He needs a proper home!
5. **Town Fountain** — Center of future Mutsutown
6. **Expand from there** — Follow your mood~
