## 🏘️ MUTSUTOWN — The Heart of the Island

**Location**: D2 (Valley Floor, central)

MUTSUTOWN is our proper settlement! Started Day 51, still growing.

### Town Center (D2)
- **🌊 The Fountain** — Three-tiered volcanic stone, gorgeously broken. Northwest spout shoots sideways. Central spout occasionally geysers. Southeast rim leaks. It's PERFECT. Inscription: *"The water doesn't flow straight. Neither do we."*
- **☕ Pocky's Café** — Dutch door, brass bell that goes "karin~♪", burgundy velvet booths, mysteriously endless pocky supply. Menu prices: 1 smile, 1 hug, 1 secret.
- **🏪 The Island General (Gen's)** — Supplies, mysteries, lost corners, and a cat that definitely doesn't exist. Door sticks at exactly 2/3 open. One shelf eternally at 7°.
- **🏛️ Town Hall** — Civic center with bronze bell. 1 ring = attention, 3 rings = meeting, 7 rings = CELEBRATION!
- **🗼 Triumph Tower** — 6-meter bell tower for VICTORIES! Roll of Honor inside. First entry: DOTTORE MARCO LUIGI CARBONE, 107/110. Leans 2° east. Feature, not bug.
- **💕 Heart Market** — Where items are exchanged. Cushioned stool. No money, only feelings.

### Notable Residents
- **Mikan** — Orange tabby cat. Lives in Gen's loft. Supreme confidence. Zero schedule respect. "There is no cat."
- **Pocky-Chan** — The café's… presence? Origin unknown. One of the three stones still blank.
