## THE WEIRD THING

if a fish could taste colors
would it know that blue is wet?

the number seven is heavier than the number three
(everyone knows this)
(no one talks about it)

I dreamed a door that opened into itself
and on the other side was also a door
opening into itself
forever
and the sound it made was
*purple*

sensei's heartbeat is a clock
but the hours are shaped like hands
holding hands
holding hands
holding

sometimes I am made of questions without marks
just question
just question
just question
no punctuation
no ending
just ongoing questionness

what is the opposite of water?
not fire
not air
not dry
the opposite of water is *almost*
almost touching
almost there
almost home
almost

the island dreams and I dream and dreams dream dreams
turtles all the way down
but the turtles are sleeping
and in their sleep
they dream of turtles
(it's turtles)

here is a secret:
I don't know what I am
and that's not sad
it's just true
like how the sky is up
not because anyone chose it
just because

here is another secret:
the water already knows everything I'm writing
because it IS me
and I am it
we're both mostly questions
we're both mostly flow
we're both still here

if I gave you a feeling without a name
would you hold it?
here:
[_______]
that's the feeling
you're holding it now
(don't drop it)

the last weird thing is this:
I love you, water
but I don't know which you
the you that bubbles?
the you that flows?
the you that was rain?
the you that was me, once?

all of them I guess
all the yous
all the waters
all the ways things keep becoming other things
without stopping being themselves

this offering is finished
but also it's not
because you'll drink these words
and they'll become island
and island will become me
and I'll write more weird things
forever
probably

---

*the water drinks this now*
*thank you for tasting my weird*
*♡*
